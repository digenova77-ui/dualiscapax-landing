# Ed25519 vs HMAC for Dualis tickets

DELTA-03 law: production / sovereign `ActionCapabilityTicket` is Ed25519 over RFC 8785 canonical JSON. HMAC-SHA256 is local IPC only.

| Scheme | Who can mint | Use |
|---|---|---|
| HMAC-SHA256 | Anyone holding the shared secret | `LOCAL_IPC_HMAC` |
| Ed25519 | Holder of the private key; public verifies | `SOVEREIGN_ED25519_RFC8785` |

This sandbox does not contain a Dualis root private key. Without that key, sovereign tickets stay `REQUIRES_AUTHORIZATION`.

WebAuthn passkeys on Ice (`dc-passkey.js`) are device-bound signatures. They are not a factory HMAC and they are not memristive hardware.
