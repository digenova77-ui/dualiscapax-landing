Park is still USDC. This page names why, and what DAI would change.

Who can stop your coins

USDC: Circle can freeze an address. Court-shaped, used. DAI: no blacklist on the token. Governance can still change the system around it. USDS, Sky’s newer dollar, added freeze hooks DAI does not have.

What sits under $1

USDC: cash and T-bills Circle attests. Redeem at Circle if you are on their rail. DAI: vaults — crypto, tokenized bills, and a large slice of USDC itself. If USDC is frozen in those vaults, DAI still transfers; the peg can wobble.

Why Dualis parks USDC
  • Processors and cards already speak USDC.
  • More chains, more books, cheaper hops for dust.
  • CAD FMV is a bank-dollar story, easier to stand on.
  • Issuer freeze is named, not hidden.

DAI as a second park would be a later slot if owner binds a DAI address and accepts protocol + USDC-inside-DAI leftover.
