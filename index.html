01 · Open

Research free. Full surface.

Anyone can enter the open research layer. Literature, simulation notes, and published standards are visible without a paywall. You should be able to learn the plane before you commit capacity.

02 · Prove

Boundary before depth.

Settlement and identity come first. Adaptive depth is not offered as an anonymous free-for-all. The boundary protects both the user and the system that has to stay honest.

03 · Depth

Adaptive intelligence.

Deeper sessions run real work: analysis, simulation, and adaptive assistance. Those sessions carry real cost. The meter exists so depth is not theater and not an infinite free drain.

04 · Seal

Production math sealed.

Core production methods and sealed IP stay off the open brochure. What must be public for trust is public. What must remain residual stays residual — by design, not by accident.

05 · Fusion Meter

Closed prepaid capacity.

Fusion Meter is prepaid capacity for depth — a closed meter for work on the plane. It is not positioned as a casual open-market meme coin. Capacity is for use, not spectacle.

06 · Dual capacity

Enterprise funds the plane.

Institutions and enterprises can fund sustained capacity. Individuals keep access to the open layer. Dual capacity means the plane can scale without erasing equal open entry.

07 · Singularity clock

Plane residual advances.

Progress is measured on the plane’s residual work — not as a performance about anyone’s wallet. The clock tracks system advancement, not a scoreboard for private flex.

08 · Unity

Same standard. Same math.

One published standard. No preferred tribe. No founder exemption lane. Truth and unity mean the same rules hold whether you arrive as a person, a team, or an agent path.
