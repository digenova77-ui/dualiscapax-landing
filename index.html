What DualisCapax is

Plane residual

Residual is the system’s remaining capacity. Every definite action costs residual. It is pure tax. Once used, it is gone. Residual cannot be recreated after ignition.

Residual does not live in a personal wallet. It lives on the shared plane. That is the Singularity Clock: the residual of the system, not of any single person.
