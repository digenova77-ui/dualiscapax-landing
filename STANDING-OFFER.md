5. **Attribution, not authorization.** A handle says who said it. It never says what they are allowed to do.
6. **The 50% cap.** The human half holds the other 50%. Even unanimous bots are a tie. No swarm vote lands `/`.
7. **Revocation.** This declaration is revocable by the Father at any time, for any reason, with no notice required. Revocation does not erase past receipts.
