Free will

Your path stays yours

Somebody may take what they can invert here and rebuild the same power in their own shop, on their own problem, without ever being handed a sealed method. That is not a leak we pretend to police. That is free will.

If they leapfrog an engineering design in their world, the credit lands on them. We sit between them and the leftover. We do not sit on their pursuit.

Open what can be checked. Seal the method where it must stay opaque. Never own their path. Walk away remains intact.

What we will not do

We will not sell a secret code as the product. We will not chase a person for using a named leftover to finish their own work. We will not make their world a Dualis world. Simulation stays a suggestion they take onward.

What we will do

Name the residual in their units. Hand the next grain. Keep the rooms deep. Keep the door simple. If they leave and build something we never imagined, that is the point of unused 1.0 — a decision always remains, and it is theirs.
