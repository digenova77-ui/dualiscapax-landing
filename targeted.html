Seats · Not a signed list

Country. Then province. Then the room that spends.

We seat national government first. Then provincial government. Then the provincial sector under that government. A name here means we think the fit is real. It does not mean a deal is signed.

01

National government

Canada. The law of the country is the first door. If the country cannot sit, the rooms below it do not pretend they can.

02

Provincial government

Ontario first in this house, because this house lives here. Other provinces follow the same step. The province holds the schools, the hospitals, and the grid. We do not skip it.

03

Provincial sector

Education. Health. Energy. The books stay with them. We measure leftover. We do not invent a diagnosis. We do not invent a signed plaque.

Signed partners live on their own page when a human stamps a deal. This page is the map of who we would ask, in order.
