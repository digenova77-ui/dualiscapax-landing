# STRIP.md

## The three boxes (universal method)

Every claim, every task, every decision passes through three boxes. No exceptions.

```
BOX 1  CLAIM      the statement, the task, the assertion
BOX 2  FETCH      a second eye reads the same bytes
BOX 3  COLLAPSE   PASS / FAIL / HOLE
```

- A claim stays rail A until a second eye fetches the same bytes.
- The fetch is not optional. It is the method.
- The collapse is the verdict. An empty seat is a HOLE, never a PASS.
- The author never verifies itself.

## What this replaces

- Any law file that says "claim is enough"
- Any workflow that asserts without fetching
- Any desk that marks without reading
- Any vote that binds without a second eye
- Any "I already know this one" exception

## What remains

- L0 (NO_FORCE, HOST_SAFE, CLEANUP_FIRST, TRUTH_OR_NOTHING)
- The four inequalities (person≠invention, look≠seal, hose≠seat, child≠Father)
- Article 2 (leave needs no reason)
- The receipt spine (verb, object, Kind, eye, seal)
- The dual pipe (rail A claim, rail B fetch)
- The Father draws. The desks mark. The street is unchanged until he sits.

## What is stripped

- Trinity as a governing body
- UCU / UVM as currency or measure
- Unity ID as a required gate
- Perpetual residual as a till
- Swarm as a census
- Any claim that cannot be fetched

## The enigma

The gap between rail A and rail B is permanent. The method does not close it.
It names it. Every hole is a place where the enigma lives, and the law is to
keep naming them instead of pretending they are gone.
