More locked than minted · Until it isn’t

Each vault must hold more than it owes.

That is the overcollateral rule. The system dollar is the sum of those vaults, plus a 1:1 USDC door.

Vault

Lock collateral. Draw DAI. Debt grows by a stability fee. If collateral value over debt falls below that vault type’s liquidation ratio, keepers sell the collateral. Penalty on top. Dust floor so tiny vaults cannot linger.

Peg door

PSM / LitePSM swaps USDC ↔ DAI at one dollar, small fee. That is why a lot of “DAI backing” is USDC. Freeze on that USDC does not freeze your DAI token. It can still lean the peg.

What Dualis does not do

We do not open Sky vaults. We do not mint DAI. Park stays native USDC. DAI overcollateral is protocol risk we would take only if owner binds a DAI address later.
