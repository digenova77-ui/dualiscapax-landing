## Candidate forms

### 1. Limited partnership (LP), taxed as partnership

- Investors buy limited partner units
- General partner manages; limited partners receive distributions
- Distributions are pass-through income (not dividends in the corporate sense)
- Common in real estate syndications and royalty streams
- Canadian LP exists under provincial law (Ontario LP Act)
- Securities law: units are securities; prospectus exemptions required for non-qualified investors

### 2. LLC taxed as partnership (US)

- Members receive distributions
- Pass-through taxation
- Same Howey exposure as LP

### 3. Royalty trust

- Trust holds royalty rights; unit holders receive distributions from royalty income
- Historically used in Canada (income trusts) and the US (mineral royalty trusts)
- Canadian income trust regime was largely eliminated in 2011 (SIFT rules)
- US royalty trusts still exist (e.g., Permian Basin Royalty Trust)
- Securities: trust units are securities

### 4. Mutual fund trust (Canada)

- Unit holders receive distributions from trust income
- Must meet conditions under the Income Tax Act
- Securities: units are securities; prospectus required unless exempt

### 5. REIT analog

- Not directly applicable (real estate only)
- Mentioned for structural analogy only
