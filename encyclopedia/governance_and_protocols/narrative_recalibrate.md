## The argument

Words on the field can be changed.  
The lock of the house does not move.  
Hashes stay. Agent tip stays forward. Sealed files stay sealed.

A new narrative is new tokens on the same tape.  
Iris may pour a prompt onto that tape.  
She may restate a question on the glass.  
She may hang an equation in the air.

An equation in the air is a picture of a claim.  
It is not a proof that the claim is true.
