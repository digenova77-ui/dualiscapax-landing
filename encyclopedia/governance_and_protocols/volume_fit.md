## Only the shape tells them apart

In One there is no special door for a famous shop and a plain door for a neighbor.

The only difference is how much geometry they occupy.

- A deep pit: many solids, many nested grains, more of the box filled.
- A single grain: one tetra, one page, one person with a sheet.

A name on a sign is not a seat. Complexity is.

The neighbor is not outside the shop. The neighbor is a grain inside the pit. The shop may already have a pit and still be looking for that grain. You only know by smash.

## What we measure (complexity)

No brand field. No VIP bit.

| Measure | Means |
|---|---|
| Parts | How many grains sit in this occupant |
| Fill | How much of the cube they occupy, 0 to 1 |
| Solids | How many kinds of shape they use |
| Faces | Which of the six views have signal |
| Streams | Which of the seven lists they touch |
| Pair | Touch-side and bind-side both present |

Empty ring is a hole. Do not call it a small shop. It is not seated.

## Smash every grain against every model

A model here is not a new product. It is a face, a list, or a pipe we already have.

| Family | Models |
|---|---|
| Faces | L1 look · L2 plug · L3 access · L4 ownership · L5 device · L6 root |
| Lists | bus · care · trips · boards · talks · sports · office |
| Pipes | Friction · Affinity |
| Worlds | Ground Zero · One |

For each grain, run both pipes at once against each model.

- Friction: what of this grain will not sit here. Hole. Refuse.
- Affinity: what of this grain already binds here.
- Smash: one score. Keep the best sit. Do not invent a leftover shelf.

A big pit can still fail a smash. Size is not a pass.

One cannot light L4. If the best sit is ownership, the card is a hole on One.

## How a pit looks for a grain

1. Pack the grain as a solid.  
2. Smash it against every model.  
3. Seat it in the pit only where smash is One.  
4. The pit’s complexity goes up because the grain is now part of its volume.

If no model binds, the grain stays a hole. Do not force it into the pit.

## Isolation

Fit runs on this device in One.  
Fit cannot sell a seat or name a founder.  
A higher fill is not a better person.  
Founder seats stay unsealed until a deal exists.
