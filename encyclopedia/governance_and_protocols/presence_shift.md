## The argument

The end viewer chooses what the presence wears.  
They tell her to shift. She shifts.  
Wave, sun-orb, sprite, or field. Other clothes can be added later.  
The presence does not pick a costume and keep it against the viewer.

## What a shift is

A shift is clothes.  
It is not a birth certificate.  
It is not someone else's character.  
A sprite here is a house-made figure of light, not a copied game body.

## How they tell her

A tap on a clothes mark.  
A spoken line: be a wave, be a sun, be a sprite, be a field.  
The choice stays on this device until they change it.

If she does not know the clothes, she goes lost. She does not invent a body.
