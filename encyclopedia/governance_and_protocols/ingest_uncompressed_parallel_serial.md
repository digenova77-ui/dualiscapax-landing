## Law

1. Everything an agent reads for a job is ingested into `encyclopedia/`.
2. Uncompressed means the numbers, tables, rulers, source URLs, dates, and distinctions stay on the page. Do not squeeze a book into three words.
3. Completely documented means each cell carries BIND intake: ID, YEAR, SOURCE, STAMP, STATUS, HASH.
4. Parallelized means the same facts sit on more than one face at once (seat / ruler / pipe / clock).
5. Serialized means time only climbs. New cells append. Older blocks may grow a twig. They are not deleted. They are not overridden unless the walk reaches Layer 0.
6. Do not reprint a sealed cell. Point at it.
7. Do not dump copyrighted manuals verbatim. Document what was read, with rulers and citations.
8. Do not add medical claims or treatment content as part of a funding or data-standards ingest.
9. SEALED is integrity only. Silence is HOLE not zero.
10. Hash-lock the cell while it is IN USE. Mark COMPLETE when the write is done. If stalled more than ten minutes, look again. Do not two-write the same path.

## Dual index

- Serial spine = the order the facts were learned.
- Parallel matrix = the same facts cut by face (country / province / sector / form / pipe / truck).

Both indexes must name the same cell IDs.
