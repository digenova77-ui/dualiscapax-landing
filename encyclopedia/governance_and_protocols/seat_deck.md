## Law

First face = picture + one keyword.  
Deeper faces = more words, smaller picture.  
No text wall on the first onboard screen.  
No org names on shallow cards.  
HPEDSB, QHC, a kitchen, a bank brand — stay off the glass until a human stamps a deal.

## Order

1. Nation  
2. Nation sector — Treasury, Health, Defence, Roads, Crown desk  
3. Province — Ontario first (this house lives here), other rooms as one card  
4. Provincial sector — School, Hospital, Grid, City desk  
5. Major house — Bank, Pharma, Plant, High tech  
6. That house’s desk — short story + books + portal  
7. Street last — Shop, Household, Club, Gift seat

Country first. Province second. The room that spends third. The street follows.
