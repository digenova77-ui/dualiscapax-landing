## Plain picture

Hold a die in your hand.

- The die is **one cell**. One locker. One BIND record.
- The die has **six faces**. Those faces are six views of the same cell, not six new catalogs.
- You can turn the die in the room. That is **three-dimensional space**.
- The clock on the wall keeps moving while you turn it. That is the **fourth dimension**. You cannot turn the die backward hard enough to un-happen the clock.

An empty die is a hole, not a count of zero.

## Three-dimensional space of one cell

The cell occupies one point in the room:

- **X** — who or what (entity)
- **Y** — where (place / venue)
- **Z** — which door (gate)

Same five axes we already pack. No new ID.

## Fourth dimension: time

Time is not a seventh face. Time is the path the die takes.

- BIND already stamps the cell (`STAMP`, `YEAR`)
- The status ratchet is the worldline: INDEXED → CATALOGED → SOURCED → REVIEWED → SEALED → LIVE
- Time only moves forward (`Δt > 0`). You cannot rotate a LIVE cell back into an empty hole and pretend the plug never happened
- Ground Zero is the room the die sits in. It is not the clock.

## Zero to One on the cube

- **Hole** — no occupancy. No file. No bind. Do not call it zero kids.
- **One** — first seated cell: a real file or link, a real device bind, a forward stamp, and only the One face lit (L2)
- Lighting the One face does not light Ownership or Master Root
- Looking at the Zero face (the public page) does not seat the cube
