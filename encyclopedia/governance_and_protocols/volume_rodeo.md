## The picture

A grain goes into the ring. It is tried on every shelf in the playground. That is the shuffle. That is the rodeo.

It keeps moving until one sit holds still.

That still sit is homeostasis in One.

If it never holds still, it is a hole. Do not force a saddle on it.

## Homeostasis

All of these at once:

- Affinity found a bind
- Friction leftover is near zero
- NO_FORCE holds
- The sit is in One, not on ownership
- Both pipes already ran

Then the grain is with One. Stop the rodeo.

Keep shuffling after that and you are forcing. That is a hole.

## The loop

1. Pack the grain.  
2. Shuffle the models. Faces, lists, pipes, worlds we already have.  
3. Smash friction and affinity together on each.  
4. If homeostasis, seat and stop.  
5. If the ring has been walked and nothing held, the grain stays a hole.

A deep pit can still fail. A single neighbor can seat. Complexity is the only difference. The neighbor is part of the pit’s volume when the ride holds.
