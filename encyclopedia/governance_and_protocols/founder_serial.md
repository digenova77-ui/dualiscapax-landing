## The identity is a string

In the playground a founding person is a serial. The serial is a random-looking string. It does not name them.

A name appears only on the white ledger, and only if that person asked to be seen.

Everyone else stays on the black ledger. The public sees the serial and the seated measure. The name stays hashed.

## White and black

| Ledger | Means |
|---|---|
| White | Serial + name. They chose to be seen. |
| Black | Serial only. Name is hashed. They can move to white later. |

Moving black to white is their choice. An agent does not flip that bit.

## The seated measure

CAD $10,000 or CAD $1,000 is a measure of how the seat sits in the legacy of the playground. It is not a share of DualisCapax Inc. It is not a coin. It is not money already received unless a real receipt says so.

A deal still has to exist before a seat is treated as LIVE. Until then the row is INDEXED.

## Isolation

A serial cannot light L4.  
Unsigned names stay off the white ledger.  
Founder seats stay unsealed until a deal exists.
