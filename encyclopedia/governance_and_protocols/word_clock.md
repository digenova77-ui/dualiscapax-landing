## The argument

Every word has a start and an end.  
The live word lights when that word begins to be spoken.  
It leaves when that word ends.  
A scrub lands on a word, not in the middle of a letter soup.

This is pinpoint at the word.  
It is not a claim that the device measured a mouth in a lab.
