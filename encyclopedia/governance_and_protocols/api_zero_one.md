## Plain picture

Two rooms. Two phones.

- **Ground Zero (0)** is the live house. People walk in and talk. That phone is **API v1**. Text. Looking. A first talk.
- **One (1)** is the playground gym. People plug a file and try the new tools. That phone is **API v2**. Sound, camera, screen, yearbook, cube cell.

You do not take the gym phone into the front hall and pretend it is the doorbell.
