## What the cabinet is

A box with two faces and a governor between them.

- Glass — what a visitor may look at. Words, pictures, a receipt that they looked.
- Motor — what may move a device, a desk, a robot, a ledger write.
- Governor — the named check. Same bit at glass, sheet, and motor, or halt.

Remainder is what is left after the check. It may stand only if the file inverts. Silence is a hole, not a remainder of zero.

## Same-door integrity

One door. The story on glass and the act at the motor must answer the same named check. A pretty glass that cannot invert is a poster. A motor that moves without a sheet is a shove.

Partners who cannot unplug do not sit in this cabinet.

## What the cabinet may hold

- Study notes that name object, stick, check, and what is out of book.
- A desk demo that returns 0, 1, or HOLE and then stops.
- Look-only research on hardware that already exists in the field.

## What the cabinet will not hold

Throne. Prize digits. Planet takeover. A signed-partner list invented from a look. LIVE status because a file exists. A motor lit because the conversation felt ready.
