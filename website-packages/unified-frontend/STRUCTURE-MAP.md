of it was adopted into this package, what was adapted to carry our

record, and a record must name what it records. The source platform's

build — `dualis-capax.emergent.host` and

spine.** There is no separate menu page and no menu widget in the
rendered text — the spine *is* the menu: roman-numeral sections in a
fixed order, each with a kicker line, a headline, and its own internal
layout. A fixed header/nav bar and any client-side route table are
**UNKNOWN** from outside — text extraction shows neither, and this map
does not invent them. Sub-pages beyond the landing are likewise
**UNKNOWN** from this seat; the landing is what was adopted.

## What this package adds that the spine does not have

Two sealed components of this package have no slot in the source
spine. They are preserved — the task that ordered this rebuild also
ordered everything sealed to be preserved — and they stand in the
open, named as additions, not smuggled into adopted slots:

- **The menu store** (`menu-manifest.json`, rendered on the face
  between VIII·B and IX): the generated categorization of everything
  the old world holds — eight categories, status and grade on every
  row, never hand-edited. The spine is the menu of *this page*; the
  store is the menu of *the whole estate*. Both stand; neither
  impersonates the other.
- **`world.html`** (the world entry, reached through the Playground
  door and the gate's success): the world's own door check, the orb
  frame (32 LOW / 32 HIGH), the Diamond World as first room, and the
  machine room. The source's landing names its world but does not
  contain it; ours travels with the package as its second page.
