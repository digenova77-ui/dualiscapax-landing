# The Unified Front End

One package, both architectures, one delivery — open license.

**What was unified.** Two builds were developed as separate tracks. In one, a 3D
world with a biometric gate: a visitor's face or finger, checked on their
own device, opens a world of rotating orbs — and inside it, the Diamond
World, where white light comes down the pipe and refracts into the
sectors the project measures. In the other, a stripped 2D front end where
the Deterministic Compute Logic Machine tells its own story in the first
person, a back end implementing the gate as a contract (challenge →
hash → the hash signs the session), a telemetry wire specced pull-first,
and a menu that categorizes the old world from a generated store instead
of hand-edited links.

The structure is the founder's hybrid timeline, drawn the day this
was built: **[2D Card Layer] + [3D Scene Layer] → one merged hybrid
timeline**, delivered as `index.html` + `manifest.json`. The manifest
follows the same discipline as the storyboard converter's output
(`manifest.json` / beats per layer / one merged timeline): every beat
of the delivered experience named with its layer — 2D, 3D, or HYBRID —
and its surface. The story beats of the film itself ride inside the
narration beat by reference to the storyboard's own manifest; this
package does not re-issue them.

This package binds them into a single delivery. Its architecture and
menu are the hosted world build's own: **a numbered spine on one
scrolling page** — adopted whole, slot for slot, and recorded in
`STRUCTURE-MAP.md`. The spine is the menu (a spine nav stands at the
top of the page); the scroll is the navigation.

- **`index.html` — the front door, on the adopted spine.** In served
  order: **II — The Dawn** (the hero: the three rings, the one law, the
  visitor's own date/time/number written their way, the heartbeat
  ticker) · **I — Before** (the story, told by the machine itself — the
  narration stands verbatim from the universal front end v2) · **III —
  The Trinity** (three cards, one per ring) · **IV — The Peg & The
  Proof** (the peg panel and the Merit panel, each graded DESIGN on its
  face) · **V — The Bind** (Unity, the three steps, the physical bind
  chain) · **V·B — The Diamond Law** (the tile sequence; the 64-bit
  word discipline stated on the face) · **VI — The Merit Economy**
  (the fruits of labor, in rows) · **VII — Live Broadcast** (the
  ledger reading, shown exactly as served) · **VIII — Take Your Seat**
  (the gate, the page's one control) · **VIII·B — Fuel the Peg** (the
  slot's law; no till) · **the menu store** (this package's own
  addition, named as such in the map) · **IX — Manifesto** (the
  closing line and the three doors).
- **The gate** is wired to the hybrid-door back-end contract
  (`~/workspace/hybrid-door/`): kick → one-time challenge → the device
  answers with a hash of the phrase its biometric unlocked → the
  challenge hash signs the session. Failures render where they happen;
  only success moves, and the redirect budget is two. On a loopback
  host the page talks to the loopback demo gate and labels itself
  DEMO MODE on its face; served whole behind the project edge, it talks
  to the same origin's gate — and says plainly when no gate answers.
- **`world.html` — the world entry.** The world's own door check
  (seated / ended / cannot-be-checked — all three rendered, none
  papered over), the orb frame with its 32 LOW / 32 HIGH header as the
  founder captured it, and the Diamond World as the first room: three
  rings, one seed, and the six sector tokens named in the world's own
  build. Below it, the machine room: telemetry bound in as a window —
  pull-first, read-only, authority none, absence shown as absence.
- **`menu-manifest.json` — the memory.** The 2D memory + menu store,
  generated at build time by `tools/build.py` (never hand-edited):
  eight categories — The Story, The Doors, The World, The Six
  Fractals, The Sectors, The Record, The Factory, The Machine Room —
  every row carrying its status and grade on its face.
- **`manifest.json`** — the merged hybrid timeline: the 2D card
  layer and the 3D scene layer as one beat sequence, generated by
  `tools/build.py` in the storyboard converter's manifest discipline.
- **`STRUCTURE-MAP.md`** — the adopted skeleton, element by element:
  what the source architecture is, what was adopted, what was adapted,
  and what was refused with its reason stated. The package's
  provenance record.
- **`VERIFICATION.md`** — the rebuild's verification: the spine
  checked item by item against the served source, the branding scan,
  the nine-check witness discipline, and the local render.
- **`BINDING-PLAN.md`** — how this package binds to the world build's
  delivery and to the founder's two domains, with Cloudflare out of
  the chain, and exactly which steps remain founder-side. Its final
  section carries the physical bind chain — reader, wallet pass,
  `POST /live/api/bind`, the core's decision, the white ledger — the
  same door, at a physical threshold.
- **`MANIFEST.md`** — every file in this package with its sha256,
  generated by the same build tool. Verify before trusting.

**The name.** DualisCapax is the only name this package carries, and it
names the architecture — not a company, not a platform, not a person.
No founder's name appears anywhere in it. No platform branding appears
anywhere in it: the page was checked the way a witness checks —
zero occurrences of the staging host's name, of any AI brand, of any
platform or copyright mark, in every file. The two truth-edits against
the v2 front end it descends from are named here, not hidden: the
footer's "no external calls" line became a true sentence for a page
that deliberately calls its own gate, telemetry, and menu store; and
the Bind door now lands on this package's own gate instead of circling
the apex.

**The law it keeps.** The story is pure — the machine speaks, no person
and no company speaks. Biometric material never leaves the visitor's
device; the phrase never leaves it either. There are no passwords.
Telemetry is observational only and degrades honestly: a surface that
does not answer is shown as NO SIGNAL, never as zero, never as a stale
value in a live costume.

**Status.** Built, verified, and pinned — **not deployed**. Placement
on the founder's domains is a separate word from the founder; the
binding plan names every step that waits on it. The living 3D world
remains the other track's build; this package binds the door, the
story, the record, and the menu to it — it does not impersonate it.
