# BINDING PLAN — the unified front end, bound twice

Status: PLAN. Nothing in this document has been executed. No DNS record,
redirect rule, Caddy block, hosted project, or box file was changed in
its making. Go-live is a separate founder word.

The package binds in two directions: **(A)** into the world build's
delivery — the lane where the gate and the living world already run —
and **(B)** onto the founder's own domains, with Cloudflare out of the
chain. Each side names what is automated, what is hand-carried, and
what remains founder-side.

---

## The Cloudflare question, settled first

The founder's diagnosis (Oct 14): *"cloudflare is the source of the
problem for the domain routing to the wrong page."* Tested against
public evidence gathered Oct 14 (DNS-over-HTTPS + the house GET
census):

- **PROVEN — .com runs through Cloudflare end to end.** The zone's
  nameservers are `aron.ns.cloudflare.com` and
  `kurt.ns.cloudflare.com`; its A records are Cloudflare anycast
  addresses (172.67.168.53, 104.21.26.28 — proxied, not an origin);
  and every .com path answers a 301 to the same path on .ai, fired at
  Cloudflare's edge. No .com request ever reaches the founder's own
  edge. For the .com half, the diagnosis is exactly right: Cloudflare
  *is* the routing, and the routing sends everyone to the wrong page.
- **PROVEN — .ai never touches Cloudflare.** Its nameservers are
  `ns1/ns2.dualiscapax.ai` — the house's own knotd on its own boxes —
  and its A record is the New Jersey box directly (108.61.17.141).
  The stale shell the .ai apex serves (an Oct 12 file whose three
  referenced assets all 404) is served by Caddy on that box.
  Cloudflare cannot explain a single byte of it.
- **VERDICT: PARTIALLY PROVEN.** One symptom — "both domains show
  the wrong page" — two causes sharing a doorway. Removing Cloudflare
  cures the .com half completely and the .ai half not at all. The
  founder's order — *resolve perfectly from the .com to the .ai* —
  requires both halves, which is why this plan has two legs.

## A. Binding to the world build's delivery (hand-carry)

The world build lives on its lane's hosts (a preview host and a
hosted project on that lane's own platforms). Those projects cannot be edited from this
seat, and this plan does not pretend otherwise. What crosses is a
**hand-carry**: the package and this plan are delivered to that lane,
whose builder adopts what fits his build. The asks, plainly:

1. **The face.** Adopt `index.html`'s story-and-gate shape as the
   face in front of his gate: the machine's first-person narration,
   the one law, and one control — or keep his own face and take the
   two rules that matter: the story is the machine's, not anyone's;
   and the only name standing is the architecture's.
2. **The gate contract.** His gate's seat section already declares
   the shape in his own words: enroll on-device, the ceremony happens
   on the visitor's device and nowhere else, the seat binds across
   tabs and devices. Our back end implements the same door as a
   contract — `POST /api/kick`, `/api/challenge`, `/api/respond`,
   `/api/cancel`, `/api/session/verify`, `/api/ceremony/enroll` —
   with one named seam: his answer is a WebAuthn passkey signature;
   ours, by the founder's order, is a phrase-hash. The contract's
   `answer_type` field carries either; the shapes around it do not
   change. His lane's answers to the front-door spec's open questions
   (Q1–Q8 in `~/workspace/rollout/HYBRID-FRONT-DOOR-SPEC.md`) remain
   the reconciliation path.
3. **The world entry.** `world.html` here is a plan view of the
   first room, not a rival world. The binding offered: his world's
   door verifies the same signed session assertion
   (`POST /api/session/verify`), so a visitor seated at the face is
   seated in the world — one seat, both architectures. His sector
   tokens (WATER · ENERGY · CLIMATE · FOOD · PANDEMIC · PHARMA, from
   his build) are the room's set; nothing here renames them.
4. **The telemetry wire.** Pull-first, as specced
   (`~/workspace/rollout/TELEMETRY-WIRE-SPEC.md`): his side GETs the
   published `/live/api/*` surfaces on its own schedule; reads carry
   `authority_effect: "NONE"`; absence is UNKNOWN, never zero. The
   handshake items H-1…H-8 in that spec are his lane's to answer in
   writing before the wire is called live. His own page already
   describes the house ledger exactly this way — "live · pull-only ·
   no identifiers" — so the contract asks him to keep doing what his
   face already says.
5. **The menu.** `menu-manifest.json` is offered as the
   categorization layer for the old-world surfaces his rooms list:
   generated, status-and-grade on every row, never hand-edited.

Nothing in this section deploys, edits, or posts anywhere. It is a
spec carried by hand, and it says so.

**What remains founder-side, exactly:** the registrar nameserver
flip (B1) — one act, his alone — and one console session on the New

after that session is automated from this seat (B5–B6), and

**What this plan deliberately does not do:** it does not route .com

whose full state remains partially UNKNOWN from this seat. And it
does not fire the older ignition or deploy-worker vehicles, which

## D. The physical bind chain (the founder's diagram, Oct 14)

The same bind, at a physical door. The founder's diagram, carried
into this plan as its physical layer — status **DESIGN**: the chain is
drawn; of its links, only the `/live/api/*` prefix is proven standing
today (the house readout: Caddy on New Jersey routes that prefix to
the live services). The bind endpoint itself is the new link this
chain proposes.

```
Physical door / reader          Unity ID wallet
(UniFi G3 Reader / Touch Pass — (passphrase + biometric binding —
 NFC / Apple Wallet /             identity proof / seeded key)
 Google Wallet)                        │
        │                               │  the pass presents a
        └───────────┬───────────────────┘  credential handle
                    ▼
        POST /live/api/bind
                    │
        Caddy proxy (dualiscapax.ai)
                    ▼
        DCLM core engine
        Iris boundary evaluation → deterministic decision
                    ▼
        White ledger
        hash-chained decision record · Twain² witness
                    │
                    ▼
        the return receipt rides back down the chain —
        the door actuates on the receipt, not on the request
```

**Guardrails, from standing law, that this chain may not bend:**

- **The wallet pass holds a credential handle, never the seed.** What
  the phone carries in its wallet — and what the reader ever sees —
  is a handle that names the binding. The seeded key and the phrase
  stay where the ceremony put them; a lost phone loses a handle, not
  an identity.
- **The white-ledger record is proof-shaped.** The chain writes the
  decision — door, time, verdict, hash — and nothing person-level.
  Who someone is stays in the dark side of the ledger's law; the
  white record proves an authorized bind happened, and that is all
  it proves.
- **The bind is an act, and its authority is stated honestly.** Every
  read path on the wire carries `authority_effect: "NONE"`. This one
  does not: `POST /live/api/bind` is a *write* — a request for a
  decision that can move a physical door. The plan names that plainly
  rather than dressing a write in read clothing. Authority flows from
  the DCLM's decision under the three rings, never from the reader,
  the pass, or the proxy.
- **Entry fails closed.** No decision, no opening — a dead network, a
  silent engine, an expired handle: the door stays shut and the
  refusal is receipted like any other verdict.
- **Exit never depends on the network.** Egress is a life-safety
  fact, not a feature: leaving a space must work with every link in
  this chain down. The chain governs entry; it is never wired into
  the way out.
- **Actuation rides the return receipt.** The diagram is the
  up-chain — reader to ledger. The door itself opens on the way back:
  the signed decision returning down the same path. A request alone
  opens nothing, anywhere, ever.

**What is not claimed:** no reader hardware is procured or configured
here; no wallet pass is issued; the `/live/api/bind` route does not
exist on the box today (the prefix is PROVEN, the route is DESIGN);
and the DCLM core's bind evaluation is this plan's drawing, not a
running service. Each of those is its own future word.
