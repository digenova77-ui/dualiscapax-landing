No deposit

Fiat becomes Fuel. Fuel is burned by the person who bought it, on the engine, or it stays banked on Dualis. It does not settle to CIBC. There is nothing to deposit. Zero or still on the book.

States
  • Credit — fiat or crypto in. Fuel on the book.
  • Burn — a pass that inverts. Fuel hits zero for that unit.
  • Banked — unused Fuel stays. Not CAD. Not a withdrawal.

CIBC is for later CAD operating cash if Dualis needs a bank. It is not this rail. This rail has no leftover sitting in a chequing account waiting for someone to spend it twice.
