Mechanics · Power

An attack should cost more than it can take.

Target: ten million times more power to throw than the thrower could keep if it worked. That is a design ratio, not a law we already proved.

How the ratio goes to the sky

If Dualis does not hold a shared pot, what they can take from the program is near zero. Cost over reward then runs away without us touching their house. That is the first invert. A payable vault of size V must be defended so the cost to empty it is at least ten million V — or the vault should not exist.

What this is not

Not a claim that SHA-256 is broken. Not a switch on their kitchen. Not a promise the ratio already holds. When a contract exists, the critique asks: what can they take, and what must they burn to take it?
