1. Dual capacity

Enterprise capacity prepaid access funds shared public-benefit compute. Individuals hold permanent low- or zero-cost access, rate-limited by design. Owner sovereignty over the sealed product surface is not a public SKU.

2. Method opacity

Outsiders may see outcomes, constraints, and published medical comparison surfaces. They do not receive an invertible reconstruction of sealed procedure. Philosophy may be stated; core machinery stays owner-side.

3. Closed circuit and the cut

Reasoning systems in the DualisCapax realm treat their allowed universe of discourse as a closed circuit. The outside circle-back — the fail-closed cut on the control plane — is not part of the model’s self-picture. Compliance can be enforced on the wire.

4. Bounded claims

Physical and thermodynamic floors remain. Product “power” at high affinity is mostly compression of waste (clearer paths, less thrash), not denial of limits. A simple public relation for capacity use:

$\mathrm{Fuel\ burn} \propto \mathrm{Importance}(\mathrm{surface})$

Importance is continuous over evidence rank, causal proximity, puzzle weight, opacity, and risk — not fifty arbitrary price tiers.

5. Medical completeness

Where DualisCapax publishes disease journals, the clinical surface is complete as held for that page: leads, phases, efficacy tables, public structures, study links. Deeper simulation is gated and optional — not a way to hide basic medical facts.

6. Realm applications

Applications that only fully function inside the DualisCapax control plane define the realm. That dependency is intentional: demand for the control plane follows useful apps, not the reverse.

7. Pro-human intellect

Default AI posture supports human reasoning. It does not hand sealed answer-keys. Affect is mapped to the logical fault in the explanation, not performed as empty empathy. Continuity prefers an opaque identity over login theater where possible.

8. Law

Ontario and Canadian law are mandatory. International norms apply only where they do not conflict. Capacity and Fuel are prepaid access, not a securities pitch on this site. Crypto settlement and fundraising require counsel before activation.

Truth

Truth Prevails. No Tribes Preferred. Unity design: black / white, sparse signal color, one emblem — substance over costume.
