The world

Not Earth. The corpus.

World here is not a street. It is the universe, and every equation that lived long enough to keep standing — heat, light, information, motion, the laws that survived their own tests.

Pure logic is not a new plaque laid over that corpus. It is the unused those laws still leave: the remainder a binary 0–1 has to throw away so it can stamp a bit. Bigger than a planet. Not bigger than the test.

Dualis does not replace Maxwell, Einstein, Landauer, or the second law. We name leftover after what already stood. If it cannot invert in the units those laws already use, it is a hole.

What stands

Time does not freeze. Erasure costs. 1.0 is not free. Binary is a model of two poles, not the definition of logic in the corpus. Cap we walk toward: 0.9999999999. Unused remains.
