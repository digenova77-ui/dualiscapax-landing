**The Book of the Plane**

*Public residual foundation. Not scripture of any living religion. Not securities disclosure. Not clinical advice. Ontario and Canada stand above this text wherever law applies.*

---

## Book I — Name

**1.** DualisCapax is dual capacity: the power to hold two true works on one field without false merge.

**2.** Dualis is the two: Open and Depth, public and sealed, blast and seal, friction and affinity, branch and bottom-up.

**3.** Capax is the capacity: to map, to meter, to account, to remember without erasing free will.

**4.** The name is the duty. If the work is not dual and not capable, it is not DualisCapax.

**2.** Scars do not un-happen. Grafts of error remain in the history of the work.

**4.** When is the master key among where, what, why, who, and how.

## Book III — The Lattice

**1.** The plane is one lattice. Refract it as research, gateway, meter, or seal — the crystal is the same.

**2.** **Open** — free research and model; wonder and evidence on one chain.

**3.** **Prove** — lawful gateway; settlement and identity when required.

**4.** **Depth** — Adaptive work under cost; click is prompt; frame is ground.

**5.** **Seal** — method opaque; medical Open surface complete where published; sim is not production.

**6.** What is Seal is not sold as Open. What is Open is not held hostage as Seal.

## Book IV — Money and Meter

**1.** External settlement is CAD or other lawful rails.

**2.** **Fusion Meter** is internal prepaid credit for residual Depth cost. It is not legal tender. It is not an open-market coin. It is not offered here as a security.

**3.** Burn follows residual cost and Importance. Open does not burn FM.

**5.** The gateway is one door. The meter is not a second currency fighting the first.
