## Loop (every room, every time)

1. HEAD the cursed stem and three candidate names.
2. Keep a candidate only if it is **404** (free). 308 = already swallowed. 200 = already a room.
3. Add `_redirects` only:

4. Do **not** replace `index.html`. Do **not** `wrangler pages deploy cf-pages`.
5. If the new name later 308s, pick the next unused synonym. Do not go back to war with the first name.
6. Real unroute (Worker = None or delete route) stays the permanent hatch. Rename is the work-today hatch.
