Onboarding

You are not betting on nothing. DualisCapax states where capacity and donations go before you participate. The waterfall is locked law of the L2 playground.

Locked donation waterfall (critical)

Any donations to the L2 Unity playground apply in this order only. Enhancements do not jump the queue. UBI does not fund a fat residual bill first in name only.

First
Cost reduction

Residual cost of the playground: edge, baseline AI (brain under eFuse), hosting, rate-limited floor. Shrink the bill so Unity can carry the mind as infrastructure.

Second
UBI / grassroots floor

Only after residual costs are addressed: shared low- or zero-cost access for individuals, rate-limited by design.

Third
Playground enhancements

New depth and features last — from real surplus, not from starving cost control or the human floor.

Incentive: keep residual cost shrinking. That is how donations reach people and, only then, better tools.

Paths

Enterprise capacity

Prepaid access credited toward agreed commercial terms. Funds public-benefit compute that carries the playground residual (including baseline AI).

Grassroots / UBI-shaped floor

Low- or zero-cost access when the waterfall and residual budget allow. Not unlimited; rate-limited.

Fuel (escalate only)

You are not paying for the brain to exist. Fuel is for voluntary deeper pull — Importance-weighted — on top of the playground floor.

AI in one line

Mind pegged to the control plane. Baseline AI = residual cost of Unity. Individual Fuel = escalate only. Brain runs under eFuse authority; the cut is not part of the model’s self-picture.
