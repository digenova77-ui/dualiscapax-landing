Not writing on the walls. A solid inside the cube. A certain triangle can stand for a whole page. An empty ring is a hole, not a count of zero.

Blue strand — the bind on this device. The gate. The layer.

A rung — both sides present. That is One.

No rung — a hole. Nothing invented.

Solids in the lower box — one shape for one phrase. Same page, same triangle.
