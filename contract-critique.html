Mechanics · When the text exists

We attack our own bind first.

High-level deals get a finished instrument. Then Dualis reads it as an enemy with time, compute, and no courtesy. Not to teach a theft. To see whether the invert holds.

Stance

Assume the reviewer wants the money, the clause, or the unwind. Assume they will try the program, the signature, the clock, the story after submit, and the court after the program. If any path returns value without a close, the instrument is wrong.

Turn the attempt
  • No shared pot — a drain has nowhere to drain.
  • Replay dies on a nonce that cannot spend twice.
  • A pause cannot become a withdrawal.
  • “I did not mean to click” does not mint a refund.
  • If recovery needs a network rewrite, the design failed before the attack.
Handoff

Owner delivers the contract text. Agents critique. Findings come back as: holds, or this invert is missing. Outside counsel still signs the legal layer. Dualis still does not claim unhackable.
