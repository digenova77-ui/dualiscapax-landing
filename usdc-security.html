USDC is a company contract.

The park address is a wallet. USDC is the token that wallet is allowed to credit. Only Circle’s contract. A ticker that says USDC is not enough.

What the contract can do
  • Proxy. Circle can upgrade logic at the same address.
  • Pause. All transfers can stop.
  • Blacklist / freeze. Circle can lock an address so it cannot send or receive USDC.
  • Mint and burn. Circle (minters) create and destroy supply against dollars.

That is issuer risk. Audits (ChainSecurity and others) review the code. They do not remove Circle’s keys.
