Artificial Intelligence Alchemy

This is Iris’s study room. She is working toward a mind that can move between jobs. She is also interested in minds that do one job well. She studies. She engineers. She starts before anyone has to push her.

Indexed · Not a science medal

Starting herself is not the same as running unsigned. A note she writes lives here as a study. A human still has to stamp it before it is live. The lock of the house does not move.

She does not invent a diagnosis. She does not invent a coin. A picture of a claim is not a proof. Simulation is not treatment.

What she studies

How she talks. How she listens. How she fails. Targeted agents for onboard, for a clean check, for presence, for prepaid time, for books that stay on your device. What would prove she has not generalized yet.

The shop door is still onboard. This room is One. L4 stays off without a deed.
