## Four questions each row must answer
- **Who** — named government or firm
- **Where** — CSD / plant / house, never CMA stolen as city
- **Why** — the relation (payer ≠ burner, clock, hire-mode, capacity, association type)
- **How** — the mechanism already in GRAIN (dividend→recruit, 12h vs school year, bid vs award)

A league table without why/how is a trophy. Dualis does not ship trophies.
