## What physicists mean
They do *not* mean Earth getting bigger, or Toronto stretching, or a quasar exploding into a void.
They mean: on the largest scales, the typical distance between unbound galaxies grows with a scale factor a(t). Light in transit stretches with a. That stretch is cosmological redshift: 1 + z = a_now / a_then.
Galaxies are not required to be flying through a fixed box. In the usual picture they ride the metric. Bound systems (you, a city, the Solar System, the Milky Way) stay bound — local gravity / other forces beat the background expansion.
Quasars and pulsars sit *inside* that picture as sources. They are not the expansion.
