# HALLMARKS — same frame, different body
2026-08-27 00:31 EDT

Oncology does not treat every tumour as one tumour. It uses a shared frame: growth, supply, limit, invasion of the neighbour, whether it answers to one system or many.
Municipalities are not tumours. The *method* is the same: one framework, many presentations.

## Shared pathways (the frame)
Every organism can show:
- growth vs land (`POP_CHG`, `DENS`)
- supply limit (`WTP.HEADROOM`, `TS.LIMIT_MW`)
- cost of keeping the body running (`TAX_LEVY`, `POLICE.BUDGET`)
- what it borrows from the neighbour (exported water, imported power, shared board)
- scar of an old join (`AMALG`)
- more than one voice in one throat (two clubs, two unions, two plants)

Those pathways can be present, absent, or silent. Silent = hole. Not "healthy." Not "cancer-free." Just unread.

## Different presentations
Atom: few organs, one water story, junior marks.
City: I-slots begin (campus, court, second plant).
Metro: many agencies, many plants, legacy names still alive inside the body.
Same pathway, different organ. TTC is not Quinte Transit with more buses. It is another transit species on the same "move" pathway.

## What the frame forbids
Diagnosing Toronto as late-stage Belleville.
Curing a hole by copying a neighbour's organ.
Calling two metros the same disease because both have a subway-class rail flag.

## Use
Read the body in front of you. Mark which pathways printed. Leave the rest. The framework travels. The diagnosis does not.
