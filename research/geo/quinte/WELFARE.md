# Welfare / social assistance — Quinte then Quinte West
2026-08-26 23:03 EDT

## What is measured

There is **one** Consolidated Municipal Service Manager for Ontario Works, housing waitlist, child-care subsidy, and homelessness prevention covering Hastings County **and** the separated cities of Belleville and Quinte West:
**Hastings County Community and Human Services**.

Quinte West is a **sub-office**, not a second system.
Prince Edward County is a different CMSM (Prince Edward–Lennox & Addington Social Services). Do not pour Picton numbers into Trenton.

## Quinte (the CMSM area)

Operator: Hastings County CHS. Head office 228 Church St, Belleville. Intake 613-771-9630 / 1-866-414-0300. Apply also at ontario.ca/socialassistance.
Offices: Belleville, Quinte West (91 Creswell Dr, Trenton), Bancroft, Madoc.
Also: Children's Services, Housing Services, Homelessness Prevention.
Hastings Local Housing Corporation: **1,473** owned units; **380+** rent-supplement / allowance slots (county site, 2026).
2023–24 Homelessness Prevention Program to Hastings County: **$6.1 million** (Ontario newsroom, 10 Jul 2025).

CHS 2024 annual report (public PDF):
- average OW cases / month: **2,104**
- average people on OW: **3,457**
- TCA average caseload: **252** (360 children)
- emergency assistance cases: **285**
- exits to employment: **284**
- calls received: **35,000**

2026 county budget (public PDF): OW benefits **$25,498,313**; discretionary benefits **$1,500,120**; employment & financial assistance programs envelope **$35,493,979**. Admin cost-shared 50% with the province.

Those counts are **CMSM-wide**. They are not Belleville-only and not Quinte West-only.

## Quinte West specifically

Same OW / housing / subsidy intake. Desk: 91 Creswell Drive, Trenton, 613-392-1387.
City of Quinte West does **not** administer OW. It points residents to the county line. It does run/partner: Housing Action Plan (2023), warming centre with the county (Dec–Mar), Community Cupboard, CSWB planning.
Pointer (Quinte West CSWB draft via civicweb): 22.1% of local renters in core housing need (2024); at least **155** people identified as experiencing homelessness in the region (Steele 2024). Treat as pointer until the named study is opened.
Ontario newsroom 10 Jul 2025: $1 million for a 10-bed St. Leonard's youth home in Hastings, cited as filling a Quinte West HAP gap.
