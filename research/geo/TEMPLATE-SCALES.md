# Template scales — Belleville is the map, not the city forever
2026-08-26 23:45 EDT
Belleville is the first city taken to depth. When the framework and the relation planes are drawn, that *shape* travels. The names do not. The next city is its own identity.

## What travels (the template)
- RANK: public books first, then hardest private, then shops
- DROP cells: complement vs bodies, hire mode, clock, plug, stay %, analog parked
- RELATIONS planes: corporate / business / public / social
- Association type **local-GC ↔ regional-hospital-family** (Belch ↔ QHC). On every later city search: find the hospital family, find the invited GCs on its capital tenders, draw the same four planes. Do not wait for the award $.
- Chamber = member index, then verify
- DATA-RULE: no invented dollars

## What does not travel
P&G’s >1,000, $3.20 premium, Belch’s Ameliasburg yard, BGH ICU 2020 bid list. Those are Belleville facts.

## Scale ladder (each rung is its own identity)
| scale | what it is | data expectation |
|-------|------------|------------------|
| **Shop / hamlet** | pin + one line | Chamber band if it exists |
| **Town** | public desk + one plant if any | levy page if they print one |
| **Belleville-class city** | this template, full depth | four public books + hardest plant + contractor plane |
| **Metro** | same planes, more shelves | sunshine lists, multiple boards, multiple hospital corporations, named bid portals |
| **Region / CMA** | do not steal the city’s book | CMA GDP and hospital-family stats stay labeled regional |

A metro is not “Belleville with more zeros.” It is a new identity with more *published* drop cells. Granularize the rung before filling it.

## Country search rule
When the query is a contractor or a hospital anywhere in Canada, first ask: is there a Belch–QHC-type association (local GC on that health family’s capital list)? Then fill the four planes. Award hole is allowed.
