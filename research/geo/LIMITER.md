# Limiter — law and paradigm as variables
2026-08-26 23:55 EDT
Statute and custom will cap every drop cell. Dualis does not accept “the law says no” as the end of the row. The row is: **why is this law the limiter here, and where is the same residual not limited that way.**

## Pattern
| residual | limiter here | not-limited there (type) |
|----------|----------------|--------------------------|
| public pay transparency | ON Sunshine / BPS expenses | a US city with no equivalent list |
| transformer capital | OEB denied Belleville TS ICM | a distributor that got ICM |
| industrial connect | DC exemption *chosen* then cut (2025) | a city that never discounted |
| hospital award $ | procurement / closed bid | a board that posts the award |
| plant headcount | private 10-K silence | P&G *did* print >1,000 on a plant page |
| contact | Sentinelese isolation law | a city that publishes everything |

The limiter is an ID: `ON.SUNSHINE.EXISTS` vs `US.CITY.NO_SUNSHINE`. Not a sermon.

## Dualis move
1. Name the limiter (statute, regulator, custom, TOS, paradigm).
2. Name a place the same residual moves without that limiter (or with a different one).
3. Relate: what residual does the limiter *create* (opacity, OT plug, analog hunt).
4. Do not breach the limiter. Engineer the Dualis layer that still measures both sides.

“Cannot because they forbid it” is the old stop. “Forbidden here, permitted there, here is the gap” is the work.
