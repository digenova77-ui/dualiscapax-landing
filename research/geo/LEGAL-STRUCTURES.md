# Legal structures — know first, relate second
2026-08-26 23:56 EDT
Hundreds to thousands of structures. Dualis does not need them to apply to DualisCapax. Dualis needs them *defined* and then *related to each other*. A relation claimed without intimate index is analog leak with a law degree.

## Law of this book
1. **INDEX** the structure as its own identity (name, source of authority, who it binds, what it hides).
2. **Do not relate** until invertibility holds: you can point at the clause or custom, not a summary of a summary.
3. **RELATE** only as typed edges: same residual, different limiter; same limiter family, different residual.
4. Explain the edge in the structure’s own words plus the Dualis ID. If you cannot show how you knew, the edge is not in the system.

## Starter families (types, not intimacy)
common-law court · civil-code · mixed · religious-personal · customary / indigenous · party-state · municipal charter · regulator (OEB, FDA) · procurement / bid-law · Crown / treaty · private TOS · closed-identity (Sentinelese) · offshore / special-zone · professional college
Each family is a hole until a named instance is indexed (Ontario *Municipal Act*, OEB Act, QHC procurement, Cloudflare TOS, …).
