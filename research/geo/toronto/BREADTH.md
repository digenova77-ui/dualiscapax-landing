## Just opened (this pass)

### Waste — `WASTE` (Belleville: small-city collection, no city-owned distant landfill)
City managed **>850,000 t** in 2024 · ~350,000 t diverted · **418,000 t** to Green Lane.
Residential diversion **51.7%** (single-fam + multi).
Green Lane (Southwold Twp, outside London) — **city-owned**, ~10 years left, close **~2035**. 2023 received ~420,000 t.
Even at 80% diversion by 2050 the city still expects ~260,000 t residual/year.
Keele Valley is closed and still a gas/RNG organ.
`LANDFILL.IN_CSD` = no. `LANDFILL.OWNED_OUT` = yes. That ID is new for every later city.

### Long-term care — `LTC.CITY`
City **directly operates 10 homes**, **2,619** beds, **97.4%** occupied.
Waitlist May 1 2025: **>7,500** applications · >2,000 first-choice city home.
Belleville: county / private / hospital-attached species. `LTC.CITY` often N/A.

### Child care — `CC.LICENSED`
**1,076** licensed centres in Toronto · **942 (88%)** in CWELCC as of Jul 2025 · 26 home-care agencies, 23 enrolled.
Fee subsidy is a city SSM organ. Belleville: county SSM.

### Film — `FILM.SPEND_$`
City film plan: direct production spend **$2.6B (2022 record)** · **$2.2B (2024)** · **$2.3B (2025)**. Top-five North American hub. **>3 million sq ft** studio. Workforce line “40,000+" is their plan voice — keep tagged.
Belleville: occasional permit. N/A as a hub.

### Bike Share — `BIKE.SHARE_TRIPS`
Open-data series: **7.8 million** trips in 2025 (from 665k in 2015). 2024 TPA projection 6.4M rides, 862 stations, 23 wards.
No Belleville analogue. N/A.
