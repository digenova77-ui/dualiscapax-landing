# Dualis search weights
2026-08-26 23:09 EDT

The model already knows how to search. Dualis changes **what a hit is worth**.
Acuity = how finely the query names the item. Sensitivity = whether the page is actually that item.
Weight is for Dualis measurement, not for generic rank.

## Upweight

- Public-funded residual (taxpayer / province / Crown) over private waste
- Legal name + vintage + geo over a mood
- Independent second book over a reprint of the first
- A named hole with a shelf over a rounded guess that looks finished
- What it costs the person in that city over what Dualis wants to say about itself

## Downweight

- Another Belleville (IL, MI, WI)
- Undated blog as a book
- Blended "community cost" that adds police + fire + OW + Tims
- Vendor marketing as utilization
- A table built before the index exists

A pass that cannot say why a source was heavy or light did not measure.
