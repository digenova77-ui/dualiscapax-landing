# THEIR-STORY — what working cosmologists actually say dark energy is
2026-08-27 01:42 EDT
Book A only. Dualis stays out.

## Short version they will defend
Late-time expansion is accelerating. Something with *negative pressure* can do that in GR. The cheapest something is a cosmological constant Λ: energy density that does not dilute, pressure p = −ρ, about 70% of the present energy budget. They do not know what microphysical thing that density *is*. They know the expansion history it produces.

## How they got there
Type Ia supernovae (1998): distant lamps fainter than a decelerating universe predicted.
Then CMB + BAO locked a flat-ish universe that needs that extra budget now.
Name: dark energy. Working default: ΛCDM.

## What they will admit if pressed
The predicted vacuum energy from quantum fields is off by a grotesque factor. Why Λ is small, and why it matters *now*, are unsolved. That is the why-gap.
Backup stories on the shelf: a slow scalar field (quintessence), modified gravity, or Λ plus systematics.

## The live plot twist in their own room
Some DESI + CMB + SN stacks prefer a shape where w was below −1 and is now above it — "weakening" in headlines. Other papers say BAO alone still sits with Λ. Not a settled overthrow. An argument.

## One sentence they own
We can fit the history. We have not derived the vacuum.
