# CLASS — not peers
2026-08-27 00:29 EDT

Toronto, Montréal, Vancouver are **not** declared peers.
They are the same *class*. They are closer to each other than Belleville is to any of them. That is all.

Belleville-size ↔ any of the three = class jump. Only A–H relationship survives. Do not multiply.
Among the three = still three books. Different statute, different transit species, different amalgamation, different airport-in-CSD test. Vancouver is not even on the Ontario 444 plate.

Similarity inside a class ≠ smash inside a class.
