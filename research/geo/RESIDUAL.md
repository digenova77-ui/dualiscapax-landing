## Why nobody has the 444-list
A single "efficiency rank" across Ontario CSDs requires, in order:
1. Existence map on every organ family (not a vibe).
2. Species match (STRONG only). OPP vs municipal police is not a number.
3. A declared residual *per family*: cost of the organ minus the service the organ is for.
4. Live pull of X at test time.
Skip 1–3 and you get a dashboard. That is why the list is empty.

## Residual is not "smaller budget"
Toronto $18.9B vs Belleville is scale, not waste.
Residual is burn that does not invert to the organ's job:
- SOGR backlog that grows while the pipe still leaks (`$.SOGR` vs `W.LOSS`)
- Treated-not-billed water (Quinte West already printed a loss)
- Owned-out landfill years (Green Lane ~2035)
- Two books that refuse to pair (TCHC corp SOGR vs city line) — the Δ.BOOK *is* the residual until they close
- Yellow-bus $ that cannot be a TTC ride
- Peak-hour paid school transport vs a free-ticket organ that does not move a bus
If we cannot name the organ, we cannot name the waste.

## "What Toronto does that Belleville could do better"
Illegal as one sentence. Legal as a list of STRONG IDs plus a criterion.
Criterion must be declared: $/ML delivered, SOGR per km, rent-core-need share, diversion %, uncollected garbage kg, vacancy vs TCHC wait — one family at a time.
Vice versa is the same gate. Belleville has no subway SOGR to "do better." That organ is ABSENT. Do not score it.
Tonight's plate can *point* at candidate residuals (TCHC two books, Green Lane clock, QW water loss, Kingston 44% rent vs own-rate drop). It cannot rank them into one index without the pulls and the formulas.

## Index shape (when layer 1+2 are complete)
Not one number. A sortable tensor:
`R[body, family, year] = f_family(X_cost, X_service)`
Sort by family, by grain, by year. Mash to one rank only as a view, never as the store.
444 rows appear when 444 bodies have HAS stamps, not when we average three congestion vendors.
