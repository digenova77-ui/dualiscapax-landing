# ORGANISM — one municipality is one body
2026-08-27 00:30 EDT

A CSD is not a row to multiply. It is an organism. You identify **markers**. You do not decide it is a small Toronto or a large Stirling.

## Markers (what we already measure)
Vital signs — A. People, levy, density, tenure, core need.
Organs — police model, fire halls, plants, pads, slips, schools-as-sites.
Metabolism — water cap vs peak, MW vs forecast, units started vs target. Headroom is health.
Immune — unions, boards, OPP vs municipal. Two unions at one fire service = two markers.
Flora it shares — health corp, school boards, LDC, conservation, housing SSM. Shared is not owned. Tag, do not swallow.
Scars — amalgamation year, legacy names still used as boxes, exported water, imported power.
Voice — club marks. Same word, two bodies (Devils / Bearcats) = two organisms inside the city, not one.

## Rule
Two organisms can share a river and still be two bodies.
A metro is not a bigger atom. It has more organs and more shared flora. Count them.
A missing organ is a hole, not a defect, unless the page said it must exist.
Inventing a marker so the body looks complete is residual.

## How you look
1. Name the organism (legal CSD).
2. Read vital signs from StatCan / the levy book.
3. List organs that printed.
4. Tag flora it does not own.
5. Leave scars visible.
6. Stop when the pages stop. That is the skin.
