# Belleville CSD — demographic shifts 2016 → 2021
2026-08-27 01:08 EDT
City CSD only unless tagged CMA. Sample-universe rows (immigration, identity) use population in private households, which is smaller than the 55,071 count.

## Headcount
Enumerated: **50,716 → 55,071** (+4,355 / **+8.6%**). Ontario +5.8%. Canada +5.2%.
Occupied dwellings **+8.3%** to 23,536. Land 247.15 km². Density **222.8 /km²**.
2025 estimate used elsewhere on this plate: **57,884** (post-census; not the 2021 count).

The city outgrew the province. That is the first shift.

## Age / generation (households)
Private-household pop **49,350 → 53,790**. Median age in that universe **44.0 → 44.4**.
First generation (born abroad): **9.2% → 11.9%**. Their median age **56.4 → 50.4** — the new first-gen is younger.
Third generation or more: **78.0% → 75.0%**.
The stock is still third-gen majority. The increment is first-gen and younger.

## Immigration (CSD households)
Immigrants **3,965 → 4,985** = **9.3%** of household pop.
Non-immigrants 88.8%. Non-permanent residents **1,015 (1.9%)**.
Recent (2016–21): **615** = 12.3% of immigrants.
Stock birthplace 2021: UK 910 (still #1, share down 23.5→18.2%) · India 635 (6.2→**12.7%**) · Philippines 370 (into #3).
Recent birthplace: **India 270 / 43.9%** of the 615 · Philippines 100 / 16.3% · US 65.
The old UK/Netherlands/Germany stock is not the growth. India + Philippines are.
CMA contrast (do not smash): CMA immigrant share 8.3%; Quinte West 7.6% / only 200 recent. Belleville city took most of the CMA's new arrivals.
