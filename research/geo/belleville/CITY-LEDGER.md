# What City Hall holds vs what it publishes
2026-08-26 23:28 EDT
The municipality has a ledger on every property. Most of that ledger is not on the website. Dualis takes the public slice and names the rest as WAIT_GRANT / MFIPPA / MPAC — not invention.

## What the city (and MPAC) actually have on a plant
- Roll number, legal description, assessed value, property class (IT industrial / LT large industrial / CT commercial)
- Property-tax bill and arrears
- Water/wastewater account: meter size, monthly consumption, fire-line charge
- Building permits: GFA, construction value, fees paid, occupancy
- Site-plan agreement, zoning, DevReady layers, DC estimate / DC paid or exempted
- Fire inspections and orders
- Planning / PAC files
- Land-sale by-law if the city sold the dirt
- Business licence only if the use requires one (plants usually do not)

## What is public tonight (city-wide, not plant-named)
- 2026 industrial tax ratio **2.40**. Large industrial same ratio.
- Industrial taxable CVA (IT) **$84.4M** → total tax ~**$4.55M** (city+edu+others in the rate by-law tables).
- Large industrial CVA (LT) **$60.3M** → total tax ~**$3.25M**.
- Those are class totals. P&G vs Kellogg vs Amazon split is the MPAC roll — not published as a named list.
- Water 2026: first 455 m³ **$2.06**/m³; next 22,275 **$1.57**; over 22,730 **$0.94**. Industrial billed monthly. Wastewater surcharge **111.9%** on the first 22,730 m³ + service, then **60%** above. Meter fixed + fire protection by size.
- Building permit industrial base **$10.00/m²**; industrial fit-up **$2.00/m²**; industrial (other table) **$12.00/m²**.
- Industrial DC exemptions **$14.4M** city-wide Jan 2022–May 2025; 100% industrial DC discount removed Jul 2025.
- Tools: Development Tracker (housing-first), DevReady (zoning + DC + permit fee estimates), CityView e-permitting.
