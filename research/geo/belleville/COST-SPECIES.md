## Species
| species | meaning | Belleville example |
|---------|---------|-------------------|
| **Standalone** | the thing itself | water $2.06/m³ first block; P&G $3.20 premium |
| **Correlated** | moves with another ID | police OT with AUTH–DEPLOY gap; fire vol-now with target 45 |
| **Derivative** | caused by another residual | $14.4M DC exempt recovered from tax/water; Elexicon pause → MD-recruit payer hole |
| **Bond-dependent** | exists because of a relation; the bond can raise or cut it | Berry ↔ Hamilton Smith / Moira / Hunter / Alliance; Belch ↔ QHC bid list; Peak ↔ Avonlough + health-hub |
| **Meaningful** | would change a decision if it moved | Belleville TS capacity; 24/7 EMS enhance $634k |

A cost can wear two tags (derivative *and* bond-dependent). Standalone still gets quantified. Unrelated ≠ unmeasured.
