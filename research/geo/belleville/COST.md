# Cost of a wasted Belleville P&G seat
2026-08-26 23:33 EDT
Rolling hire is a public fact. “Nobody stays” is not measured here. Quantify the seat. Do not invent a washout %.

## Locked inputs (Belleville postings)
- Term associate: $18.28 + $3.20 nights = **$21.48/hr** on nights. 84 hrs / 2 weeks = 42 hrs/week → ~**$47k/yr** nights if the year is worked.
- Technician: $26.47–27.50 + $3.20 → ~**$62–64k/yr** nights.
- Canadian replacement cited in HR press: ~**$30,674** average. Manufacturing calculators: ~**75% of wage** + ~4 months to full productivity. First-45-day share of turnover often cited ~20% (industry, not this plant).

## One wasted associate seat (order of magnitude, not a P&G invoice)
| piece | low | high |
|-------|-----|------|
| Recruit + assess + PPE + fork ticket | $1.5k | $5k |
| Paid hours before they quit (1–4 weeks × 42 hrs × $21.48) | $0.9k | $3.6k |
| Buddy / trainer drag (same hours × a tech rate) | $1.1k | $4.6k |
| Line gap until the next body is live | $2k | $8k |
| **Seat total** | **~$5.5k** | **~$21k** |
Canadian “replace an employee” $30k sits above that if you add lost output and a second recruit. Use **$8k–30k per early exit** as the band. Not a plant filing.
