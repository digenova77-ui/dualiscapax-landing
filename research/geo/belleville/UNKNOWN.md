# Sector look-for vs P&G unknown bucket
2026-08-26 23:37 EDT

**Law:** Never assume the burn-rate number. Assume only that a like firm *has like characteristics*. The job is to find the actual number. Until it is found, the cell stays in the unknown bucket.

Infer **structure**. Do not infer **dollars**.

## Allowed
Because this firm is 24/7 production in this park, *look for*:
- rolling or cohort hire
- a shift that fails people (nights / 12h / weekends)
- a first-year or term or seasonal layer
- contractor / 3PL overflow
- city ledger lines (tax, water) that exist even if unpublished
- energy as a hole until a bill exists

If their public file adds or clarifies a row, write the row. If it sits sideways, leave it sideways.

## Forbidden
Do not plug P&G’s $8k–30k seat, 1,000 headcount, or $3.20 premium into Kellogg or a shop “because industry.” That is an assumed number. Park it.

## P&G unknown bucket
Anything that is a real cost line with no figure that would change a decision:
- Belleville 90-day stay %
- class size / sessions per year
- energy kWh / $
- water m³ / $
- named MPAC / tax bill
- contractor count now
- Vision or co-pack contract
- sister-plant stay % that would explain this floor

Deepen the *list*. Do not fill the cells with guesses. Revisit the bucket when another plant’s public file makes a cell mean something.
