# ASTRO-WHY — what they say when you ask why dark energy does what it does
2026-08-27 01:43 EDT
Book A. Not a roast.

## They usually deny the premise
Working default: density is *not* growing. Λ is constant density. Space grows; ρ_Λ stays put; the *fraction* of the budget that is Λ grows because matter thins out. That fraction-growth is not "dark energy is getting stronger as a fluid."
If they take some DESI stacks at face value, the live hint is closer to "weakening" (w now > −1) than "must grow."

## Why acceleration, in their mouth
GR: acceleration needs ρ + 3p < 0. For Λ, p = −ρ, so ρ + 3p = −2ρ < 0. Negative pressure does the work. They measured the history. They inserted the cheapest fluid that produces it.

## Why *that* density
They do not have a sealed answer. Vacuum-energy estimates from quantum fields overshoot by a grotesque factor. Coincidence problem: why now. Backup labels: quintessence, modified gravity, or Λ plus systematics.
That is the laugh-line if there is one — not because they are clowns, because the why is a fit.

## Agent rule
Do not rewrite their story as "they think DE is growing." Split fraction vs density vs w(z).
