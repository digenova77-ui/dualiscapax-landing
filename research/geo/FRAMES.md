# Frames — better *for what*
2026-08-26 23:54 EDT
A city chart without a frame pretends one residual is virtue. Dualis does not pick a civilization. It names the frame the metric is *for*.

## Three frames (examples, not a ranking)
| frame | what “better” means | do not smash into |
|-------|----------------------|-------------------|
| **Corporate-legal** | incorporation, capital lock, limited liability, predictable courts | a human-expression score |
| **Social-fabric** | speech, mobility, public books, labour voice | a 10-K margin |
| **Closed-identity** | North Sentinel — their law is theirs | a development index |

USA-as-corporate-frame and Canada-as-fabric-frame are hypotheses to *test with IDs*, not slogans we ship. Sentinelese sense is the identity lock we already use on cities: do not overwrite their book with ours.

## Rule on the compare ladder
Every row carries a frame tag. `BEL.BPS.AUTH_DEPLOY_GAP` under a public-safety frame is not the same row as “corporate cost of policing.” Same number, different question.
If two countries only share a *type* (water exists), they do not share a frame. Type travels. Frame stays labeled.

## Dualis position
We measure residual inside the frame the owner named. We do not declare which frame should win the world. Residual-to-zero is our frame: less wasted search, less smashed viewpoint, less analog leak. That is not a claim that every society should look like Belleville.
