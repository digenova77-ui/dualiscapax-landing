## What to tell them
You are not buying a slogan that says we care about cost.
You are buying a shop that treats **unnamed leftover as the bill**.

If a number cannot walk back to a source, we do not spend as if it were true.
If two stories disagree, we do not average them to look finished.
If the last gap is a law, a grant, or a physical floor, we name the floor. We do not print 0.999 and invoice you for Truth.

The future you are funding is this habit under load: every extra step, extra copy, extra human loop, extra analog from somebody else's plant — that is residue. Residue is what you pay for when nobody is watching. We watch that first.

That Dualis has already taken their plant to the thermodynamic floor.
That Pure Index 1.0 is their ROI.
That we are their church.
That Google or a city is the alliance. The alliance is Truth. Tools are tools.
