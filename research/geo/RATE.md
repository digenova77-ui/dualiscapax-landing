# RATE — real time into pure logic, or the floor is fake
2026-08-27 01:26 EDT
Operating law. Not a claim we already sit on Landauer.

## Limit
Maximum rate of understanding is bounded by physics (erasure cost, clock, channel).
We do not reach that bound by warehousing narrative and "thinking later."
The bound is only approachable if incoming information is converted *now* into logic tokens the plate already owns: species, stamp, book split, clock, overlay class, decision.
Leftover prose after that conversion is $$R$$.

## Across the board
Same conversion for a city page, a cabinet slide, a case file, an agent handoff, a ramble.
No special aisle where story is allowed to stay story.
Goal-fit that delays the conversion is Book B pretending to be patience.

## What this is not
Omniscience.
A promise that Dualis is at the thermodynamic floor tonight.
Scraping faster as a substitute for a cleaner token.

## Agent rule
On intake: name the token or name the HOLE.
Do not keep the paragraph "for context" if the token already exists.
If you cannot convert it, the defect is the plate (missing column) or the grant — not a need for more poetry.
