## Why V9 exists

The instinct is right: people will pay a premium for *cut through the bullshit* decision work.

The mapping is wrong if that premium is pasted onto Leaf $49 / 12 mo.

- SuperGrok Heavy sells a **general model seat** with a weekly usage pool.
- Dualis Leaf sells a **named door format** (one indication / one sheet *shape*).
- Dualis Fuel sells **runtime** to compute a granted answer.
- Dualis Crown sells **wet-ink** chairs. Never agent.

Raising Leaf to Heavy's monthly number recreates the speech bug already rejected: ALS / MS / "the whole thing" for a subscription. V9 splits the product so the premium sits on the rail Heavy cannot sell.

## V9 rails (proposed)

Three rails. Three receipts. Never one blob.

### Rail A — Seat (the door)

Annual stays a discount, not the only cadence. Copy must say *door / format / names*, never *oracle*.

| Seat | V8 CAD | V9 CAD / 12 mo | Optional monthly cadence | Honest sentence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leaf | 49 | **149** | 19 / mo | One named Look-plus-workbook *shape* |
| Branch | 299 | **790** | 79 / mo | One clade of names + Measure |
| Trunk | 499 | **1,800** | 179 / mo | One class pack of names |
| Atlas | 1,499 perpetual | **4,900** perpetual | — | Taxonomic index of names only |

Look / Measure stay **CAD $0**. That is the 100-logical-truth front door. Do not charge for the question.

Leaf at $149 / 12 mo is still a format. It is not Heavy. It is not a sealed monograph.

### Rail B — Decision / Fuel (the premium)

This is the Heavy analogue, except the unit is a **granted answer on one class**, not a weekly chat / Imagine / voice pool.

| SKU | CAD | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Fuel F1–F5 | 5 / 20 / 50 / 120 / 350 | Burst runtime. Keep. |
| **DEC-M Decision month** | **450–900 / mo** | Named class + Iris-on-catalog + Fuel pool that does **not** share Imagine. Throttle = `SUM(units)` after class grant. |
| **DEC-D Decision desk** | **2,400–4,800 / mo** | One instance focus: one legal name, one pack, walk-back on *their* meters. |

Decision month should cost **at or above** Heavy (~USD $300 / ~CAD $410) because it is narrower: one chair, one class, no riddles, no cure speech, no surprise weekly cliff on video.

Receipt always two lines:

1. Seat (door)
2. Fuel or DEC-* (clock)

Mixing those two lines is how $1,499 became "the whole thing."
