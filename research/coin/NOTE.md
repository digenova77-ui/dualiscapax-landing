# Residual contract is not the coin

`DualisResidual.sol` is a signed term between a client, Dualis, and an attestor. After a measured save it splits a pot: client keep rises from 81% to 100% over four years, remainder to a Dualis address.

eFuse is not that split.

- No Dualis payee
- No pot
- Fixed supply to a founder
- Company is media

Keep the residual contract in this folder as history. Do not deploy it as eFuse.
