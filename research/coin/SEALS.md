# Seals

Not a valuation of DualisCapax. DualisCapax is worth nothing on a book.
eFuse is not issued. These doors are paper until two watchers see inflow.

## Rule

Each finished work (medical run, engineering drawing, the next thing) is a seal.
A seal has a tag you wrote, or no tag.
The playground has one house meter.
A gift may name a seal. Then only that seal's meter moves.
Untagged money is a gift to the house.

Two locks, both have to click:

1. Ready — the work is the thing you claimed. A full pot does not promote a draft.
2. Funded — two watchers see seen inflow ≥ that seal's tag.

Unlock is for nothing to the named kitchen class (lab, sponsor, CRO that can take the next step). Not a shop. Not equity. Dualis does not become the trial sponsor by opening the drawer.

Ladder: cheapest ready seal whose tag is set opens first. Bigger tags wait.

`$15M` was spoken as an example. It is not set on any seal.
Do not print tags on the live hall.
