## Sequence

1. Set genesis and founder. Write them on this plate.
2. Deploy `EFuseToken.sol` to the chosen testnet only. Label the address TESTNET.
3. Run a transfer from founder to a second test address. Watcher records the hash.
4. Two agrees: supply matches genesis, transfer hash is seen twice.
5. Only then may a page say "a testnet unit exists."
6. Mainnet is a later day. Trust Wallet listing is after mainnet, after circulation is real, after their review. Brand new tokens are not accepted there.
7. Flip `issued` only on mainnet after the same two checks.
