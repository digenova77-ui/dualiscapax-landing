An attack is a throw. We do not raid.

Each binder closes their own doors the same way we close ours. That is access denial. It is not a strike.

Residual invert: the leftover of the throw, not their treasury. No shared pot ships. What they can take from this coin is near zero.

## Bind

A Unity ID that wants the pact signs:

`Unity bind <sitting> armistice close-own-doors`

The plate stores the sitting, the proof, and `live`. Revoke flips `live`. Coins do not move.

## Tagged spend

A bind names a leftover. A gift can wear that name. Unified money is one purse of tagged lines, not a company pot.

If you give to this pact you name the leak. That gift stays on that leak until the leak closes. We do not spend it on something else. We do not spend it on a raid. DualisCapax Inc does not skim it. The token contract does not hold it.

Adequate means keep closing that leftover until it is closed or the tagged line is empty. Not a slogan percent. Not ten million times.

If a gift arrives with no leftover name, it is just a gift. No allegiance spend is claimed.
