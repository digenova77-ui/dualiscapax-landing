Full report

1. Scope

Public ALS disease-modifying context with SOD1 tofersen (VALOR) endpoints parallel to riluzole and edaravone. Not a substitute for primary publications or labels.

2. VALOR spine

Miller et al., N Engl J Med 2022;387:1099-1110. SOD1-ALS, 2:1 tofersen 100 mg vs placebo, primary ALSFRS-R at week 28 in faster-progression subgroup: −6.98 vs −8.14 (diff 1.2; P=0.97). Biomarkers (CSF SOD1, plasma NfL) reduced vs placebo. OLE 52-week early vs delayed ALSFRS-R −6.0 vs −9.5. LP-related AEs common; neurologic SAEs 7% tofersen recipients.

3. Model constraints

  • Heterogeneity forbids one-molecule universal claims.
  • ASO success does not transfer to all ALS without new evidence.
  • Open face refuses cure language.
