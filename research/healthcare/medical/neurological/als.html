Where research stands

ALS is progressive motor neuron disease with limited disease-modifying options. Riluzole is the long-standing broad standard: classic pivotal framing often cites about 2–3 months median survival extension; multi-center real-world residual (PRECISION-ALS class) reports treated vs untreated HR near 0.70 and larger median differences in some cohorts. Edaravone slows ALSFRS-R decline in selected cohorts and formulations. Tofersen is SOD1-ALS only — biomarker engagement is clear; functional primary at 28 weeks was not significant; open-label early-start residual favors benefit inside that genotype. AMX0035 / Relyvrio followed an accelerated path then Phase 3 miss — graft-risk exemplar. Broader Phase 2/3 pipelines remain mixed.
