Where research stands

ALS remains a progressive motor neuron disease with limited disease-modifying options. Riluzole and edaravone are established standards with modest effects on survival or decline in selected groups. Tofersen addresses SOD1-ALS with clear genetic targeting. Broader Phase 2/3 pipelines continue; results are mixed and trial design is hard.
