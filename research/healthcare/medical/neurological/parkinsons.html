Where research stands

Levodopa (with carbidopa) remains the most effective motor-symptom therapy. Continuous subcutaneous foslevodopa/foscarbidopa (24-hour infusion) was FDA-approved for motor fluctuations (2024). Extended-release oral formulations target OFF time. Disease modification remains an open residual — symptomatic control is strong; inversion of progression is not established in public SoC.
