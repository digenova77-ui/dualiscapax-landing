Blueprint (not piggyback theater)

Piggyback stack: combine prior approved mechanisms and literature blocks → can approach very high coverage of known axes (your 99.99% hypothesis as a ceiling on recombination of existing blocks).

Residual difference: each historical graft of a treatment paradigm onto living disease leaves failure modes that time does not rewind. Those graft scars are permanent in the patient timeline. A residual model that only remixes prior blocks without accounting for irreversible graft failure will stall under the last basis points.

Parallel compound exploration: classical high-throughput + optional quantum-inspired or small-circuit sandbox sims may explore many candidates; x86 “quantum simulator” is not a physical QPU and does not grant infinite simultaneous chemistry. Scale is FM- and hardware-bound.
