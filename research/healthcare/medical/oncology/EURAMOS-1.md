# EURAMOS-1 — public spine for OST
2026-08-26 22:16 EDT

NCT00134030 · ISRCTN67613327 · AOST0331 · EudraCT 2004-000242-20
Phase 3 · open-label · international (17 countries, ~326 centres)
Registered Apr 2005–Jun 2011: **2260**. Randomised: **1334 (59%)**.
Eligibility: newly diagnosed resectable high-grade osteosarcoma, age ≤40.
All registered patients received preoperative **MAP**, then surgery. Post-op assignment split on necrosis.

Good histologic response: <10% viable tumour.
Poor histologic response: ≥10% viable tumour.

Primary endpoint on both randomisations: **EFS**. Secondary: OS, toxicity.

## Poor-response arm (Marina 2016)

MAP (n=310) vs MAPIE (n=308). Target had been 693; 618 randomised.
Median follow-up 62.1 months.
EFS events 153 vs 154. Deaths 101 vs 92.
EFS HR **0.98** (95% CI 0.78–1.23). Hazards non-proportional (p=0.0003).
3-year EFS: MAP **55%** (49–60) vs MAPIE **53%** (47–59).
Localised 3-year EFS: 60% vs 57%. Metastases at registration: 24% vs 18%.
First event was usually metastatic (~85%).
Febrile neutropenia without documented infection: 50% MAP vs 73% MAPIE.
Authors: do not add IE after poor necrosis — toxicity without EFS gain.

## Good-response arm (Bielack 2015)

Good response reported in 1041/2260; **716** consented to randomisation (MAP 359 vs MAP+peg-IFN-α-2b 357).
IFN 0.5–1.0 μg/kg weekly after chemo until 2 years post-registration.
271/357 started IFN; 105 stopped early. Grade ≥3 on IFN in 133/268 with toxicity data.
Median follow-up 44 months. 3-year EFS all randomised: **76%** (72–79).
EFS events 93 vs 81. Adjusted HR **0.83** (0.61–1.12; P=0.214). Not significant.

## Dualis read (M, not a label)

Both add-on arms failed to lock extra compression. Poor-response intensification is the graft exemplar already scored MAPIE 40 vs MAP 58 in osteo.dual.v2. Good-response IFN is a second unpaid add-on (compliance residual). Necrosis grade is a classifier, not a license to pile agents.
