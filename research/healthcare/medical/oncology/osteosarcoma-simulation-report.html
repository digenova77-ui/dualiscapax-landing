Full report

Public SoC is MAP plus resection. Historical surgery without adjuvant chemo left systemic residual unpaid (EFS ~11% on the Dualis leaf). EURAMOS-1 MAPIE did not improve EFS (HR 0.98) and added toxicity — intensity is not compression. Localized OS band does not transfer to unresectable metastatic disease.
