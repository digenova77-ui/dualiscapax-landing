Where research stands

HGSOC accounts for most ovarian-cancer deaths. Public biology: near-universal TP53 mutation, homologous-recombination deficiency in about half of cases (germline or somatic BRCA, BRCA1 methylation, other HRD). Standard first-line residual is primary or interval cytoreduction plus carboplatin–paclitaxel. Maintenance then splits on genotype and residual disease: olaparib after response in BRCA-mutated disease (SOLO-1 class), niraparib with a broader HRD / all-comer label path (PRIMA class), olaparib plus bevacizumab in HRD after PAOLA-1-class combinations. Platinum-sensitive recurrence still answers platinum again; platinum-resistant residual is thin. Public literature still records recurrence in a large majority of advanced cases within three years. That recurrence, not the first scan, is the residual this leaf meters.
