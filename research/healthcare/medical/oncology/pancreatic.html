Where research stands

PDAC remains among the highest-friction solid tumors. Public 5-year OS is still cited under ~12%. Drivers: activating KRAS in >90% (G12D / G12V / G12R class), plus a dense desmoplastic stroma that can be most of the mass — high interstitial pressure, poor perfusion, drug delivery as a physical residual. Lineage is not one disease: classical (GATA6-high) versus basal-like / squamous (GATA6-low, EMT-high) with worse public prognosis and easier escape under stress. Front-line systemic residual is FOLFIRINOX or gemcitabine + nab-paclitaxel in fit metastatic disease; both relapse. A minority HRD subset (BRCA1/2, PALB2) has a platinum and PARPi maintenance path (POLO class for germline BRCA). That subset is not the all-comer floor. Stroma and basal-like switch are the residuals that survive after the first cytotoxic row.
