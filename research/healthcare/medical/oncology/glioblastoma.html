Where research stands

Glioblastoma remains the highest-friction primary brain tumor in public residual. Standard of care is maximal safe resection, then radiotherapy with concurrent and adjuvant temozolomide (Stupp). Original Stupp median OS was 14.6 months with RT+TMZ versus 12.1 with RT alone. Modern trial-selected newly diagnosed bands are often cited ~15–18 months. MGMT promoter methylation stratifies TMZ residual; unmethylated disease keeps a thinner public benefit. TTFields added to maintenance TMZ improved OS in the EF-14 class (~20.9 vs ~16.0 months in that comparison). Bevacizumab moves PFS more than OS in many newly diagnosed settings and is a graft-risk row when sold as survival. Recurrence residual is near-universal.
