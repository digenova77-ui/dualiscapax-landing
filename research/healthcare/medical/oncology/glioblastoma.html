Where research stands

Standard of care remains maximal safe resection followed by radiotherapy with concurrent and adjuvant temozolomide (Stupp). Median OS in trial-selected newly diagnosed cohorts is often cited ~15–18 months; TTFields added to maintenance TMZ improved OS in the EF-14 class analysis. Recurrence residual remains severe. Not a cure surface.
