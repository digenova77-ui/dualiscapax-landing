Where research stands

Osteosarcoma is the most common primary bone sarcoma in adolescents and young adults. Since the 1970s–80s the backbone has remained neoadjuvant / adjuvant MAP (high-dose methotrexate, doxorubicin, cisplatin) plus surgical resection. Localized 5-year OS is commonly ~65–70%; metastatic disease remains far worse (~20–30% EFS when resectable).
