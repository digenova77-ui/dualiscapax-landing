# Public platform — claim that can be checked
2026-08-26 22:45 EDT

The shelf is any **public** place a hard claim can be pointed at later: URL, docket, clip with a date, PDF, post on a named account.
Reddit is one such place. So is X, a trade site, a hearing stream, a municipal PDF, a conference deck they posted.

Private Slack and a rumor with no handle are not shelves.

Rule stays serial: record the claim → hunt an independent second → echo of the same mouth is not a second → unconfirmed stays M-possible or pointer-only.
The platform does not upgrade the rank. The independence of the second cite does.
