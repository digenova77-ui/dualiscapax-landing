# Instance focus — DCLM at one name
2026-08-26 22:28 EDT

Basic DCLM binds two constraints.
Focused DCLM does not leave the named instance until that instance is scored in its own units.

It is not a general intelligence that glances at a sector.
It is attention that refuses to look away from one legal name.

## Law

1. Load one instance.
2. Fill its meters from public P1, then from what they grant.
3. Deepen the same tweak (AHU-12 under that plant; school 14 under that board). Do not hop to a sibling to feel busy.
4. Industry / class objects exist only as later aggregation of scored children.
5. Stop claiming "we understand them better than they do" until walk-back passes on the meters they actually run.

"Better than they understand themselves" means: their systems already hold the pieces and do not coordinate them. The row is the coordination. It is not mind-reading. It is not private data we were not granted.

## What this is not

- A vibe about the company
- A sector slide with their logo on it
- Studying everything else as a substitute for studying them
- Babysitting after the page

If the instance is not yet understood, keep granularizing *it*.
