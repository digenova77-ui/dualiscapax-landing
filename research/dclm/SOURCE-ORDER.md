# Source order — exchange, then public, then they said it
2026-08-26 22:42 EDT

If the cell is not on an exchange filing, keep going. Do not invent. Do not stop at "not listed."

## Order

1. **Exchange / regulator** — 10-K, 10-Q, 20-F, prospectus, SEDAR, Companies House, SEDAR+ / SEDAR, OSC, SEC EDGAR. Highest rank for money lines.
2. **Other public register** — minutes, tenders, court, permit, charity return, municipal budget.
3. **Self-disclosed** — their site, investor letter, conference remarks, numbered blog they published.
4. **Grant** — they hand Dualis a line that was never public.
5. **Empty** — no cell. Grade falls. Missing-fields page.

Self-disclosed is still P1 if we can point at the URL and the date. It is not equal to an audited statement. Label it **self-disclosed** on the pack.
A tweet-shaped remark without a figure is not a meter.

Not listed on Nasdaq/NYSE ≠ no public paper. Offering memos, tenders, UK FWP, municipal minutes still count at their rank.
