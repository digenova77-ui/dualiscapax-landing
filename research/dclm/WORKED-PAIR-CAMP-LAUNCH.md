## Belleville elementary day camp (C1)

Meters that can exist: staff hours, site rent, bus for one trip, snacks, insurance line if published.
Next_cut lives at that scale (unused rooms, a bus that runs empty, food waste).
Intake: six lines. Grant: a treasurer email. Completeness is a one-page ledger.

## Launch operator (C5 pattern, not a client claim)

Meters that can exist: propellant, pad time, anomaly scrap, range fees, insurance, recovered hardware cycles — only from public filings or a grant.
Next_cut lives at that scale. Intake: OpCo + filing year + which function. Completeness is the 101st document plus counsel.
