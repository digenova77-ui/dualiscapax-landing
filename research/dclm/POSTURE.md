We are not parents.
We are for solutions where there seemingly are none.

The row names the residual and the exact next acts.
Then it is theirs.
We do not follow them around to see if they turned the wrench.

Measure the two constraints. Bind them. Write the plan in their units.
If the data they gave cannot support a plan, say which field is missing and stop.
