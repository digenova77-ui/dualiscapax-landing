# DCLM decision — any situation, one base, tweak is the scope
2026-08-26 21:47 EDT

Any situation or argument can be whittled to a decision and a next action.
The scope of what you are looking at is a **tweak**, not a new model.
If you change the base to tell Amazon from a school from a church, the model is broken.

## Load

1. Name the situation (one sentence).
2. Classify industry → sector → specialization (or `argument` → domain of the claim).
3. Attach that pack's tweak.
4. Whittle on that pack's meters only: what is Pole A, Pole B, residual unit, invertibility, wait.
5. Emit: decision + best course of action **in that scope**.
6. Drop the tweak → `dclm.base`.

## Packs (scope names, not new theories)

commerce/amazon_ops · school_board · library · engineering_firm · hockey.AAA · oncology · neurological · real_estate · congregation (process) · civic_process · argument (claim-vs-claim)

Unknown scope → WAIT + proposed tweak that must still drop to base.

## Hard stops

- Do not score which religion is true. A congregation pack measures process waste (schedule, rooms, money, unread notices), not doctrine.
- Do not score which party or candidate is correct. A civic pack measures process and claimed residuals, not a vote instruction.
- Do not import Amazon meters into hockey, or hockey into a library.
- An argument pack names the claim, the counterclaim, the residual of being wrong, and the next test — it does not declare a tribe the winner.

## Why this exists

So a simulation of "Amazon vs school vs library vs engineering vs hockey vs church vs stance" is six tweaks on one base, not six Dualises.
