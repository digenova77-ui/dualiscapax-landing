# Invalidation — future facts rewrite the rank
2026-08-26 22:30 EDT

A pack is true at a vintage. It is not true forever.
If a closed-loop finding lands in the real world (a therapy, a drivetrain, a tariff), the rank of residuals can flip. History of advice is then stale. That flip has a dollar sign.

## Law

1. Every next_cut carries a vintage.
2. An oracle on that instance watches public surfaces that can move those meters.
3. When a new P1 fact lands, re-score the instance. Do not append a newsletter.
4. If the first residual changes object (HVAC → transport), the printed plan from last month is withdrawn as first cut. It may remain a lesser cut.
5. Tell them the rank moved. Do not babysit the wrench. Do not charge again to say the rank moved if we already watch that instance.

## Shock pattern (not a claim)

"Someone closed ALS" or "someone made a corridor 100× cheaper" is an exogenous P1. Medical or engineering — same rule: cite, unit, date, invertibility. Then re-rank the named instance that used the old physics.

Do not invent the shock. Do not leave the old HVAC sentence standing as if the world had not moved.

## What this costs us to build

Named-instance oracles. Vintage on every cell. Re-rank as a first-class act, not an annual refresh.
We are not there on every entity tonight. The law is already this: when the fact exists, the old first cut is not allowed to pretend it is still first.
