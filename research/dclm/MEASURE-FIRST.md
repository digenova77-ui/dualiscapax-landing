# DCLM measure-first
2026-08-26 21:56 EDT

Define the tweak of the thing in front of you first.
Do not pre-define a sector or industry measurement and pour the firm into it.

## Order

1. Identify what this instance actually is and what fields it has.
2. Mint **this** tweak. Units from this dataset. Absent functions stay absent.
3. Repeat for the next instance of the same kind of work.
4. Only then assess the pile of tweaks. Shared filled meters may rise and become a sector object.
5. Shared sector objects may rise to industry.

Going up is aggregation of things already measured.
Going down from a pre-written "retail model" is graft.

## Slots vs scored generals

An empty index row (`tweak.business.financials.bank`, `tweak.sport.ice_hockey.NHL`) is a **slot**. It does not score. It does not claim to know the meters.
A scored general exists only after at least one child tweak has identified its meters and a smash has shown what survives.

## Default run

Internal residual of the named thing.
Industry assessment only if they asked, and only across tweaks that already exist as identified children — not across empty slots.
