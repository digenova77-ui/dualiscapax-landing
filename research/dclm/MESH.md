# Data mesh ↔ Dualis gaps
2026-08-26 22:05 EDT

Dehghani's four principles (necessary together):

1. Domain-oriented decentralized ownership
2. Data as a product
3. Self-serve data platform
4. Federated computational governance

A product is discoverable, addressable, trustworthy, self-describing, interoperable, secure.

## Dualis map (not a claim Dualis already is a mesh product)

| mesh | Dualis |
|------|--------|
| domain | named instance + its tweak (Walmart ≠ Amazon ≠ a board) |
| data product | asset ID + meters + units + provenance (P1/P2/M) + drop path to DCLM |
| self-serve platform | onboarder playground + actuator — still WAIT_GRANT on several rails |
| federated governance | DCLM invertibility, isolation, DUA-1, Master gate, dual-TOS. Global rules. Domain decides its meters. |
| mesh edge | **the gap** Dualis owns: two records of the same thing no vendor will hold together |

## What Dualis must not do

A central Dualis lake of every Canadian sole prop is the opposite of mesh.
Copying Amazon's cost constraints onto Walmart is the opposite of domain ownership.
An empty industry blob scored as if it were a product is not a product.

## Honest bound

Principles are adopted as law for how packs and joins are built.
The self-serve platform is not live autonomy tonight.
