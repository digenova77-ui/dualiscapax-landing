## Custody

They keep their books.
We need to **see** the lines the tweak uses. We do not need to live on their servers. We do not need to compute inside their environment.

Source feed stays theirs.
Dualis may hold a working copy only long enough to issue the dated pack, then the working copy goes. What remains on our side is the issued pack they already received (vintage, cites, next_cut) — not a warehouse of their ledger.
We do not sell their feed to another tenant.

## Honesty

If a line is missing because they fear handing it over, the model is limited by that fear, not by Dualis compute.
The problem they sat down to solve stays unsolved at that meter.
That is not a threat. It is the completeness grade.

We model the residual they named. We do not use the feed to parent them or to shop a sibling.

## Not

- "Give us your network and we will run there"
- Keeping their store as Dualis inventory
- Promising a fiduciary duty the Crown did not grant
- A better cut than the paper they allowed us to see
