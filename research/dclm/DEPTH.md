2026-08-26 21:59 EDT

DCLM is the base. The advantage is not a homepage lecture on the universe.
The advantage is a tweak so complete that the firm's own finance team would recognize the meters.

## Target for a public company

A Dualis tweak of that firm should, from **public** record plus any granted instance feed:
- name every reported segment and what it does / does not operate
- split residual they control (A) vs chain they do not book (B)
- map expense and capital the way the filing maps them
- keep restricted-purpose dollars restricted
- hold weights empty until filled
- drop to DCLM

If their FP&A could look at the tweak and say "you missed a segment we report," the tweak is not deep enough.
If Dualis invents a meter their books cannot see, that is graft, not depth.
