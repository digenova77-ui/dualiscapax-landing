# Completeness grade — A–F on the record we can use
2026-08-26 22:34 EDT

Grade the *paper*, not the company.
How much of the meters this instance's tweak needs is already on a public filing or a document they sent.
We do not demand private books. We list what is missing and what the pack can and cannot say until they volunteer it.

## Scale

| grade | meaning | what the pack may say |
|-------|---------|------------------------|
| A | needed meters present, dated, walk-back passes | numbered next_cut in their units |
| B | meters present; one secondary line thin | first cut + named gap |
| C | identity + one coarse spend line (e.g. total opex) | direction only; no fake percent |
| D | legal name + little else usable | missing-fields page; no savings claim |
| D− | name plus noise (marketing copy, no figures) | same as D |
| F | cannot even bind the legal entity to a filing | identify only; do not sit as if a model ran |

Inferred cells from *their* public words are allowed if labeled **inferred**. Dualis-invented percents are not.

## Before the chair

If grade ≤ D, send the missing-fields page with the intake. One list:

- meter we need
- why the cut cannot be named without it
- public place it might already live (10-K note, minutes, energy dashboard)
- or: volunteer this file

They can ignore it. Then the meeting pack stays at that grade. We do not invent a "maybe 5% somewhere."

## Hunt first

Oracle on the named instance before we ask. If the 101st document already holds the line, the grade rises without them lifting a pen.
Do not mail a demand for a figure that is already in their annual report.

## Not

A moral score. A credit rating. A threat.
It is only: can we name the cut, or only the fog.
