# Principal claim — X hard number protocol
2026-08-26 22:43 EDT

Applies to every instance, not only SpaceX.
A hard figure posted by a **validated principal** of that instance (the named officer's real account, not a parody) is public self-disclosed P1.

## Steps

1. Confirm the account is the principal (handle history, not a lookalike).
2. Extract only the hard datum (number, unit, date, what object). Ignore the rest of the thread.
3. Cross-reference: exchange, register, prior letter, docket.
4. If a higher-rank filing **contradicts** it at the same vintage, the filing wins. The post is kept as a note, not the meter.
5. If nothing corroborates it, **do not delete**. Store as **M-possible**: principal-claimed, unconfirmed, still in the conglomerate.
6. The pack may use M-possible as a candidate next_cut only with that label. It may not print as if audited.
7. A later filing or grant promotes M-possible → confirmed, or kills it.

## Why keep it

The principal may be publishing the latest number before any shelf does. Throwing it out makes Dualis limited by "not yet on EDGAR." That unknown is then a hidden variable.
Keeping it as possible makes the unknown **named**.

## Conglomerate

The instance tweak is the union of every ranked cell we have: filing + register + self-disclosed + M-possible + empty slots listed.
Empty slots stay listed. That is how the unknown stops being a silent limiter.

Tesla posts do not fill SpaceX meters. Same person ≠ same instance.
