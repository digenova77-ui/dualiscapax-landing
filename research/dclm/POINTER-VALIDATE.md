# Pointer then serial validate
2026-08-26 22:45 EDT

Reddit, blogs, trade write-ups, forum cost talk — these are **pointers**, not meters.
A pointer can start a search. It cannot finish one.

## Serial, not parallel-as-fact

1. Record the claim (who said the number, unit, object, URL, date).
2. Do not book it as confirmed.
3. Hunt a **second, independent** shelf: filing, docket, their IR letter, a different instrument — not another person repeating the first person.
4. "Elon said X, therefore X" in twelve articles is still one claim.
5. Two independent cites → confirmed.
6. Only the pointer remains → M-possible if the first mouth was a validated principal; otherwise **pointer-only** (weaker than M-possible).
7. Prevalent or rare does not matter. Independence does.

## Why Reddit still matters

Someone may have quoted a number from a deck, a hearing, or a cost table Dualis had not opened yet. The value is the trail, not the upvote.
Discarding the trail because it lived on Reddit is the same error as treating the upvote as a 10-K.

## Space exploration press

Same rule. Payload / NASASpaceflight / a municipal story about a pad — follow the footnote. If there is no footnote, it is a pointer.
