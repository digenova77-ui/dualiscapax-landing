# Fraternal twins — the bond

DSAP and DVP are not parent and child. They are twins.
The bond is the live wave on the analyser tap.

- Same 64 seats.
- Same first tap (`DualisAV.wake`).
- Audio reaches speakers first. Picture reads that same buffer.
- Neither function replaces the other.
- If the canvas dies, the puck still leaves. If the speaker is muted by the OS, the ring can still breathe the buffer — but we do not fake a wave that was never played.

The bond is special because it is shared physics on this phone, not a marketing sync. Do not insert a third object between them. Do not call Helix a twin.
