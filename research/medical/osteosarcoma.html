Then the disease

Osteosarcoma

Cancer that starts in the cells that make bone. Most often in teens and young adults. Most often in the long bones near the knee or shoulder. Under a microscope the cells make immature bone (osteoid). That is the name. It is not a Dualis diagnosis.

Localized disease has a better outlook than disease that has already spread. Treatment in the real world is a team: oncology, surgery, imaging, pathology. Dualis does not treat. Dualis does not sell a cure on this page.

Terry ran because cancer hurt people. The leftover of that run is research money and a yearly hop still taken in his name. If you came here for a journal, start after you have heard him.
