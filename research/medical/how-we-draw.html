Discretion

How we draw him

We do not draw his face in quarks. We do not clone his throat. We do not wear the jersey. The photograph on the tribute is theirs (SFU, CC BY 2.0). The voice is theirs. Dualis owns only the field.

What a sprite is allowed to be

A sprite here is a leftover with a job. Not a cartoon of a boy. Not a mascot.

  • Quark — one unit of the mile. Small. Cold light. Assembles a letter he already said, or sits on the path as one step.
  • Path — St. John’s to Thunder Bay as a line of hops. East is birth of the run. The break is where the cancer returned. Particles after the break are the Foundation. They keep going. He does not.
  • Cadence — one particle in the line hits heavier and later than the rest. That is the prosthetic as physics, not as a drawing of a leg.
  • Word — letters gather from void and lock. No fade-in of a finished sentence. If it cannot assemble, it is not shown.
What is forbidden

A Dualis-drawn portrait. A synthesized voice called “him.” A 1.0 plaque. Age as the cause. Dualis inheriting the hop. Ranking Canadians. Selling the field as treatment.

Purity

Not dependent on age. The field is the unused he did not keep for himself. Dates stay dates. The photograph stays credited. The Foundation stays the living mile.
