Editorial method

How we draw

These pages separate historical facts, attributed quotations, and the site's own interpretation. Metaphors such as “the mile” or “the third pole” are editorial framing, not medical measurements or scientific classifications.

Medical facts should be checked against primary or authoritative medical sources. These pages are not a diagnosis, treatment recommendation, clinical decision aid, or substitute for a qualified clinician.

Where a source is unavailable or a claim cannot be verified, the claim should be narrowed or marked uncertain rather than promoted to fact.
