The difference

A computer can order the hops. It cannot sit in the bed the night before the cut. That leftover is named here so we do not pretend we closed it.

What he was found with

Osteogenic sarcoma in the right knee. Eighteen. Three days. Leg gone. Twenty to fifty percent, as his mother remembered the room. Sixteen months of drugs. Children in the same fire. That is the input. A model can hold the input.

What a model expects next

Minimize pain. Protect the remaining body. Stop adding load. Survive the number. That is the cheap path: infection already spent him, so spend nothing else.

What he did

He inverted it. After the infection had already taken the limb, he chose more pavement. After the ward, he asked for a dollar from every Canadian. After last place in Prince George, he aimed at an ocean he might not live to see. He did not wait to be well. He did not wait for a better number. He turned private leftover into a public hop for people who were not him.

That inversion is the difference. Diagnosis is a fact. Reaction is a will. What he made is still moving after the body stopped.

What this page cannot do

It cannot feel the prosthesis hit the Trans-Canada at 5 a.m. It cannot be the brother saying why you. It cannot be why not me from inside an 18-year-old who wanted to teach gym. Dualis can keep the order honest so a first search does not flatten him. Feeling stays human. The mile stays his.
