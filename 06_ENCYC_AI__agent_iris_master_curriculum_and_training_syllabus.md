2. Detailed Course Syllabi by Academic Tier

* Course 101: Context-Free Grammar & Automata Theory
   * Objective: Absolute compliance with formal language grammars (JSON, SQL, EBNF, YAML).
   * Dataset: 50,000 synthetically perturbed schema compliance challenges.
   * Graduation Exam: Generate 10,000 consecutive deeply nested JSON data transfer objects (DTOs) with zero syntax parsing exceptions via logit-level DFA masking.
* Course 102: Exact Fixed-Point Arithmetic & Conservation Laws
   * Objective: Eradication of floating-point drift, compounding rounding errors, and hallucinated calculations.
   * Dataset: Multi-currency ledger balancing, high-precision asset conversions, zero-sum matrix settlements.
   * Graduation Exam: Pass the 1,000-Step Zero-Sum Treasury Settlement stress trial with exact integer/cent precision.
* Course 103: Propositional & First-Order Predicate Logic
   * Objective: Translation of natural language business rules into formal first-order logic assertions ($\forall x, \exists y, P(x) \implies Q(y)$).
