Not one machine

We run the quote. We do not pretend to be Visa.

A “crypto processor” is five jobs. Dualis can own some on day one. Buying the rest is cheaper than building a bank. Ten markets is leftover. Three good ones and a reject rule is the floor.

Own
  • Invoice + watch + Fuel credit. BTCPay-class or our watcher. Hierarchical xpub. Confirmations. Credit Fuel from CAD FMV. Fee: hosting, not 1%.
  • Quote. Median of 3–5 liquid venues (Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, one DEX aggregator). Reject if they disagree more than 1% or depth cannot fill the ticket. That is the on-the-fly price. Not an average of twenty empty books.
Rent until volume inverts
  • Cards / Interac / world fiat. Banxa or MoonPay. Dualis cannot be the card network.
  • Long-tail token → USDC. NOWPayments-class or a DEX aggregator API. We do not warehouse every ERC-20.
  • KYT screen. One vendor API on the inbound hash. Cheaper than building Chainalysis.
Why not 20 markets

BTC and ETH already agree across a handful of liquid books. Adding fifteen thin DEX pools does not make the price truer. It makes it noisier. Long-tail with no three-source quote is not Fuel. It is leftover. Same dust law: if the hop cannot invert, park USDC.

Expense vs function at zero revenue

Self-run majors (BTC/ETH/USDC receive + our quote + Fuel book): dollars a month in hosting. Building card + 300-token swap + AML program: FINTRAC VC-dealer work, travel rule, LVCTR, a compliance officer. That does not invert on a $20 pack. Crown decides the exact MSB line. Agents do not.
