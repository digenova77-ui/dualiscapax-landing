The living hop — Terry Fox Foundation

After Thunder Bay, the run did not end. The Foundation keeps the mile. Dualis credits that organization for every year it has kept cancer research in public sight in Canada and abroad — not as Dualis work, as theirs.

February 4, 2026 — World Cancer Day — the Foundation announced it had surpassed $1 billion raised for cancer research, from millions of volunteers and donors finishing what he started in 1980.

  • National charity honouring his vision while funding research through the Terry Fox Research Institute (est. 2007).
  • More than 20,000 volunteers. About 3.5 million students. Nearly 10,000 fundraising events a year in Canada.
  • Terry Fox Run, every September, since 1981 — the year he died.
  • Terry Fox School Run — thousands of schools, the largest youth hop in the country.
  • International Runs — dozens of countries; money raised abroad almost always stays in the host country for local research.
  • Ride of Hope — started 2015 by Darrell Fox. Pedal hop. Same name. Same leftover.
  • TFRI — peer-reviewed teams; Research Grants; Marathon of Hope Cancer Centres Network; Digital Health and Discovery Platform. Vancouver head office. Hospitals, universities, and centres coast to coast.
  • International distributions for research outside Canada.

That is awareness with a ledger. Dualis does not claim it. Dualis points at it.
