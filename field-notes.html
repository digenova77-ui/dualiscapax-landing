Methods shelf

Use these when Dualis cites a rail. Name the assumption.

Jack and Suri 2014, AER 104(1) Risk sharing, not a Science paper. Panel ~2,282 Kenyan households, 2008–2010. Users insulated when a shock hits (~7% drop for non-users). Mechanism: remittances, more senders, farther. Variation: agent-network expansion treated as supply-side. Not an RCT.
Suri and Jack 2016, Science Later horizon. Same identification family. Headline ~194k / 2% of Kenyan households. Contested (Bateman, Duvendack, Loubere). Read both rooms.
Alinaghi 2019, 3ie Replication Paper 22 Push-button and pure replication of 2014. Core risk-sharing scar mostly reruns. Minor drift. Extra: matching, Tobit, urban/rural. Rural-wins-harder does not cleanly invert. Her poverty wrap is her step, not 2014.
Rosenbaum / Rubin 1983 Propensity score balances measured x. Hidden u walks through. Overlap required.
Rosenbaum bounds Γ After matching: how fat a hidden lean must be before the print dies. Γ = 1 is the matched lottery you wished. Report Γ*, do not invent one for Alinaghi from this desk.
Kunda 1990 Accuracy goal vs directional goal. Direction still tries to look public. Illusion of objectivity is the tell.

Leftover rails (not Dualis products)

If wealth moves toward the unbanked, uneducated, forgotten — use a door they hold. Dualis is not the issuer.

Graduation / ultra-poor Asset + short eat-money + coaching + savings nudge. Multi-country RCTs printed consumption and assets after the program ended. Heavier than a lone loan.
VSLA / savings groups Neighbors invert their own pot. Outside top-up can ride their ring without replacing it (Uganda experiment). Training + mobile-money how-to can move use inside groups that already exist.
Mobile money door Phone ledger + cash agent. 2014: shock buffer. 2016: long-run fight. Needs agents. An app with no cash-out is RAM.
Microcredit alone First-generation RCTs: investment up, mean poverty miracle no. Do not sell a tiny loan as leftover-home.
