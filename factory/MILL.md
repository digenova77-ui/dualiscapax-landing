## Size

`clades × countries × L1–L5 × debate 1–7 × 32 seats`

20 × 80 × 5 × 7 × 32 = **1,792,000** slots.

Each slot is one DCLM question on one named tweak. Cite or `awaiting_*`.
Never invent a roster, a hospital, or a player to fill a slot.

1. `factory_mill.yml` every 15 min computes `slot_id = tick % 1792000`.

3. If a P1 cite exists in git/Drive title, mark `lifting`. Else `seed` + hole name.
4. Do **not** push `main` HTML. Artifact / stdout only (Pages 500/mo is Clock B).
5. Humans + merge-plate pick from `factory/mill/QUEUE.json` when a slot deserves a door.
6. Clock B only if FPVVW word is all ones and ≥90 min.
