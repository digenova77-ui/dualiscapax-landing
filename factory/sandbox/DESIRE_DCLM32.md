## Collapse

Everybody on Earth will not want the token. Forcing that is a farm.

What can be desirable without a lie:

- **Unity / Look** — a desk you can open, check, and leave. $0. This is the wide door.
- **The coin** — only for humans who accept: lock energy, get paid when a number moves, leftover goes home, same rate, no sitting prize.

Three reasons a human actually touches the coin:

1. They already used the desk and saw a leftover fall.
2. They want their energy on that close, not on a printer.
3. They can hold it in a wallet they already trust.

Every other reason (status, APY, Satoshi costume, saving the world by buying the story) fails at least one of the 32 seats.

Affinity +1 is the desire engine: after it was already fair, one more close still happened, and anyone can read that on Look.

If a seat wants the framework and not the coin, that is success. NO_FORCE.
