# COMPLIANCE_FLOOR

Stay legal in every jurisdiction we can **name**. If we cannot name it, it is `awaiting_counsel` — not "probably fine."
This is not legal advice. Dualis ≠ coin. Look $0. No token in company ads.

## How "all jurisdictions" works

We do not scrape Earth's statute book and pretend we finished.
We keep a ring list. Each ring is either **cited** or a **hole**.
A new country on the desk (a teacher, a plant, a paid slot geo) adds a ring before we speak there.

## Rings we can define today

| Ring | Why it is real | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ontario / Canada | Home desk, OSC/CSA, PHIPA/FIPPA, advertising | **Cite** — follow CSA 21-330 / 51-356 / 31-369 spirit: no misleading crypto-style promo, disclose paid relationships, no gambling urgency |

## Hard rules that travel

1. Company ads = architecture. Coin invisible. No wink, no "if you know."
2. No celebrity / famous-name halo we did not get in writing.
3. Agency praise is house voice, not a stranger review. Say so if it is public.
4. No student PII, no phrase in git, no health record on GitHub (PHIPA posture).
5. No return / APY / "invest" claims. Residual ledger only states numbers we measured.
6. No paid clap farm. Paid slot for DualisCapax the company only.
7. Personal profit → buying the coin like Doge stays in a personal wallet, not on the poster.
8. New jurisdiction = new hole until counsel or a cited local rule is on this page.
