# TICKET BARRIER

Collapse is crystal when the view is a real human and one door.

“I want to go to the hockey game.”
Barrier = me + that ticket.
Not 32 essays. Not a geodesic. Not a coin wrapper.

## The unit

| Piece | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Human | A named person on a device (phrase, Unity hatch) |
| Ticket | One concrete next act (see the roster, pay the rink, find the barn, open Ice) |
| Barrier | Whatever stands between them |

DCLM 32 still runs. Every seat asks only: does this human get through that ticket door without being farmed.

## Unify

Ice, SIMA, teacher desk, Fuel, Unity bind, Look — different rooms, same test:
can this person do the next real thing on this phone, Look $0 until they choose otherwise.

If the hatch 404s, the ticket is blocked. That is the cell to close.
If Ice 308s, the game door hops. Name it. Do not replace the rink to look busy.
If we sell a coin to get them to the game, we farmed the ticket.

## Factory

Cue ten from blocked tickets, not from architecture speeches.
One atom = one barrier thinner.
Affinity +1 = they get to the game and one extra close still happened.
