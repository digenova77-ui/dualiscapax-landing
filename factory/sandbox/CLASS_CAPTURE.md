# CLASS_CAPTURE

Sara may keep a record of her room **on her device** if authority is real.
Dualis does not get the file. Iris does not post. Look never plays it.

## Authority is not a vibe

"Not posting publicly" is necessary. It is not sufficient.
Ontario classrooms: board policy + identifiable students → usually consent.
Holes we name:

- `awaiting_board_policy` — HPEDSB recording rule she can point at
- `awaiting_parent_grant` — per-child share grant already OFF by default
- `awaiting_purpose` — assessment / safety / her own pedagogy — not a reel

If any hole is open, capture stays locked.

## What Iris is allowed to do

On-device, after phrase + checklist:

- Noise / pace / "the room got loud after recess" — environment
- Screen of *her* lesson materials she captured
- A note she dictates about the lesson, not a dossier of named kids

Iris is **not** allowed to:

- Upload audio/video to Dualis, coin, Look, or a factory bucket
- Face-match or name children from pixels
- Score a home from a recording
- Talk in a stadium voice over a Grade 2 room

## Where the bits live

IndexedDB / her Downloads. Wipe the device = gone.
Receipt hashes *that a capture existed + duration*, never the waveform.
