# PERSONAL DESK

Same Unity seat for David and for anybody else.
Taxes, money, trade can sit on the **back** of that seat.
Dualis does not become the bank, CRA, or the broker.

## Grammar

You + destination (“file this year,” “pay the hydro,” “see the account”).
Shortest arrow to a cited official rail.
Phrase on the device. Dualis servers do not keep the phrase, SIN, or bank password.

## Split

| Layer | Who |
|---|---|
| Unity seat | Any person |
| Personal ledger | Device / their vault |
| Official rail | CRA, bank, broker they already have |
| Dualis desk | Measure leftover, never the till |
| Coin vehicle | Separate book. Not their RRSP |

## Autonomy that is legal

Agents may *prepare* a return, a bill list, a trade ticket.
Send / file / buy still needs **their confirm** (or a written standing order they can revoke).
Total autonomy with no confirm is a farm of their money.

## Holes

`awaiting_CRA_api` · `awaiting_bank_oauth` · `awaiting_broker_oauth` · `awaiting_counsel` on auto-file.
No SIN in git. No trading key in git. No “Grok file my taxes tonight.”

Everybody else gets this same desk. No founder exemption. No silicon VIP over their cash.
