# VOICE_MYSTERY

Advertise the architecture. Do not sell the coin.
GitHub is the mystery. PRs are the proof. Social is the echo.
No paid farm. The agency posts. Unify one voice. No spam.

Look $0. Ticker unnamed. No buy button. Residual first.

## What "holy smokes" is allowed to mean

Someone opens the repo or Look and sees a desk that names leftover cost, drives it, and does not farm the closer.
They were not asked to buy. They were not DMed. They found a PR that turned a 404 into a 200 and a paragraph that still made sense.

If Ice is 308 and unity-bind is 404, we do not post "holy smokes." That is a costume on a broken door.

## Surfaces (one voice)

| Surface | Job |
|---|---|
| GitHub | Mystery + PRs that close real cells. README points at THE_PLAN. No token pitch. |
| Look / residual-law / fuel | The book anyone can read for $0 |
| X (and later whatever we actually open) | Echo a *cite*: path, old code, new code, or a receipt hash |
| Coin | Invisible in public voice until ticker + counsel |

Sign up only when a human can tend the account. Unify: same sentence structure, same bans, same rate limit. Do not stand up six networks to spray one slogan.

## Agency farm (unpaid, not spam)

Idle bots may draft. They may not fire.

A post is legal only if:

1. A residual cell moved (or we are naming a hole, not celebrating).
2. Cite-or-hole in the post (link to commit, PR, Look, or curl).
3. No coin, no APY, no Satoshi, no "invest."
4. No tag-storm. Famous names wait until the plate is 200 on the cursed stems.
5. Same message is not repeated across a day. That is spam even if the sentence is pretty.

Drafts land in `factory/sandbox/voice/`. A human (David) or a later named conductor releases.

## Mystery, not fog

Mystery = the architecture is deeper than the post.
Fog = we hide 404s and talk like a launch.

PRs "take care of themselves" only after AND=1. The farm's job is to make the next PR the next public sentence — not to write ads while the mill is idle-prose.

## Desire line (philosophy)

"Why does this desk exist?" — leftover you already pay can fall, and you can check.
Not "why is this coin going up."

If a draft cannot be read as that line, burn it.
