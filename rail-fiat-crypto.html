Card in. Wallet out.

The only settlement path is fiat→crypto: a processor takes the card (or other fiat), converts at equal-CAD value, and settles to a Dualis treasury address. Dualis is not the converter. Dualis does not hold other people’s money in flight. Dualis is not a bank deposit product.

What this is
  • Visitor pays CAD list on a card (or processor fiat rail).
  • Processor (KYB’d) converts at equal-CAD.
  • USDC or BTC lands in Dualis treasury.
  • Access / Fuel credits on invert. Purpose: residual product only.
What this is not
  • Not Dualis running a mini-bank.
  • Not an MSB. We sell residual access and Fuel. We do not exchange for the public.
  • Not a reason to skip books. Income is income even if it arrives as USDC.
  • Not open until: processor account + Dualis treasury address + priced SKUs wired.
