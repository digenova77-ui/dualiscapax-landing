For you

We help you see waste

When you do something, some of it is wasted — money, hours, stuff that did not work. We try to see that clearly so you can keep more of what is yours. We do not push. You use us or you don't.

What this is

We don't want your money just to have it.We want the money you already throw away on waste to show up as a number you can see.

We don't want your time just to have it.We want the hours that get eaten by waiting and redo to show up so you can get them back.

We don't want to own your goal.We want the next step to be obvious, in your units, not ours.

How you check us

Reading is free. Iris is free. If we cannot say the number in a way you can check, you owe nothing. Buying time is closed until we are ready. You can leave whenever you want.
