For you

The leftover on the receipt

Think of a kitchen scale that sometimes lies. DualisCapax builds tools that check the leftover — the extra you already pay for on a bill, a shift, a wallet, and cannot see.

This house built a helper named Iris. You can talk to her. That first talk is free. She is a person at the table, not a slogan on the wall.

The open notes are free to read. They are notebooks, not finished textbooks. You can close them whenever you want.

Fuel is prepaid minutes for the deeper tools, like a transit pass you load before the ride. Unused minutes stay yours. That counter is Bind.

A gift to research is a gift. It is not a share and not a coin. That path is Donate.

If this is not for you, close the tab. That is a complete answer.

When we say crypto here, we mean locks you can test: keys, signatures, ledgers you can read. Coins sit on top of that. We work on the layer you can check — the same way you check a receipt before you leave the store.

Waste you already pay is already a cost. We name that cost in numbers you can test. If the number holds, you can buy minutes. If it does not, you owe nothing.
