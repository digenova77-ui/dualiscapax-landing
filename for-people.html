For the people who pay

You paid. The work should show up.

Taxes and premiums already left your account. DualisCapax builds tools so a government desk, a clinic, a school, a shop, or a household can see where that money leaked — and send what they recover back into the work.

This is not a poem. A case worker is drowning in files. A hospital bed is blocked because the discharge form is in the wrong queue. A city plow sits because the part is on backorder from a contract nobody can read. A family gets help in the spring and a bill in the fall. Those stories stack into the millions. We start with the sheet the desk already keeps.

Iris is a helper you can talk to. The first conversation is free. Ask her in plain language. If she answers in riddles, tell her to stop.

The open notes are free to read. They are notebooks, not proof that a treatment works, and not a claim that DualisCapax has already saved a ministry a billion dollars.

If you want the deeper tools, you can prepay minutes. Unused minutes stay yours. That path is payments. A gift to the notebooks is Donate. Neither one is a share or a coin.

If this is not for you, close the tab. That is a complete answer.

Money in this country moves through ledgers: payroll, benefits, procurement, tax credits. When we say crypto we mean locks and signatures you can test, the way you check a receipt before you leave the store. We do not need a slogan to do that.

Waste you already pay is already a cost. We name it in numbers you can read out loud. If the number holds, you can buy minutes. If it does not, you owe nothing.
