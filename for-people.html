What we work on

Waste you already pay — on a bill, a long shift, a plant line, a wallet. We name that cost in numbers you can test. If the number holds, you can buy minutes. If it does not, you owe nothing.

When we say crypto here, we mean locks you can test: keys, signatures, ledgers you can read. Coins sit on top of that. We work on the layer you can check.
