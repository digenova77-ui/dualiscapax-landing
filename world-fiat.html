EUR · USD · JPY · CNY · anything the card already is

They pay in theirs. You credit Fuel.

CIBC does not convert Euro or Yen. Dualis does not convert Euro or Yen. A processor that already speaks those currencies takes the card or the local method, turns it into USDC, and Dualis credits Fuel from the CAD value at that second.

Two doors that skip CIBC
  • Card / local fiat. Visitor billed in EUR, USD, JPY, CNY, whatever their bank already uses. Processor FX + settle to Dualis USDC. Fuel on the book.
  • Crypto they already hold. Any major coin in. Processor or direct send. Same Fuel.

The leftover is their bank’s FX and the processor’s cut. Named, not hidden. Dualis is not an MSB. Books still mark CAD at purchase.
