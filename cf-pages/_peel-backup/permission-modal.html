Microphone Access Guidance

DCLM Layer [0] Law Floor (NO_FORCE): We never activate your microphone automatically without explicit consent. Audio is processed ephemerally in RAM and immediately zeroized (0.00% retained PII).

Your browser or device currently has microphone access disabled. To speak to Iris:

1. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions.
2. Tap Microphone and select "Allow only while using the app".
3. Return to Chrome, tap the address bar tune icon, and ensure Microphone is set to Allow.
