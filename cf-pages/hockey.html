SEC-01 · Hockey · OHF

Five leagues. One provincial weekend.

Each league runs its own season and its own championship. Nobody nests inside OMHA. The unifying tournament is OHF.

  1. League season — the year that belongs to that member.
  2. League championship — last weekend still inside the member book.
  3. OHF — the provincial meeting. For OMHA that road is often called Red Hats, then OHF.

OHF youth members

OMHA

Ontario Minor Hockey Association. Most of southern and central Ontario outside the Alliance and GTHL maps. U16 here is a draft-year lens.

ALLIANCE

Minor Hockey Alliance of Ontario. Own zones, own championship, then OHF.

GTHL

Greater Toronto Hockey League. Not an OMHA zone. Own book, then OHF.

NOHA

Northern Ontario Hockey Association. Own map, own championship, then OHF.

OWHA

Ontario Women’s Hockey Association. Girls and women. Same OHF roof, own pathway.
