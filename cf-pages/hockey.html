SEC-01 · Five leagues · OHF

Ontario is huge. We seat the five.

Dualis does not start and stop at OMHA. The product is five member leagues under the OHF. Each owns its map and its championship. The provincial weekend is shared.

  1. Your league — OMHA or ALLIANCE or GTHL or NOHA or OWHA. Not a nest under one of the others.
  2. League championship — still inside that member book.
  3. OHF — where the five meet.

OMHA

Ontario Minor Hockey Association. Large southern and central map. One of five — not the whole province.

ALLIANCE

Minor Hockey Alliance of Ontario. Own zones. Own championship. Then OHF.

GTHL

Greater Toronto Hockey League. Own book. Never an OMHA footnote.

NOHA

Northern Ontario Hockey Association. Distance is the product, not an afterthought.

OWHA

Ontario Women’s Hockey Association. Girls and women. Same roof, own path.
