À La Carte

One place for the athlete. Hockey first — same idea for other sports.

DualisCapax is Canadian. We build seats for real kids and real families — not another app that makes you copy stats into boxes.

Hockey is our first live framework. It shows how a seat works: claim who you are on the roster, then keep next game, travel, tape, and school on one screen. Golf, football, soccer, basketball, and more plug into that same idea when they are ready. We do not pretend they are live before they are.

What we model is simple. Your kid already has TeamSnap for who showed up, GameSheet for the score, and tape for the play. Dualis brings that into one seat and helps you see what parents already notice on the glass — like a kid who fades late in the third — from real games and real tape, never from made-up numbers on a landing page.
