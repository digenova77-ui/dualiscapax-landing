Want a wallet for optional prove?

Dualis does not require a crypto wallet for the $0 / 90-day seat. If you choose Advanced → prove, your browser can talk to a wallet you already trust. We do not sell wallets, take referral fees, or prefer one brand.

Browser extension wallets

MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Brave Wallet, and others inject into Chrome/Edge/Brave. After install, return here and tap Connect wallet.

Mobile wallets with a dapp browser

Trust Wallet, Rainbow, and similar apps include an in-app browser. Open dualiscapax.ai/onboard.html inside that browser, then Connect wallet. (Example names only — Dualis has no partnership.)

No wallet is fine
