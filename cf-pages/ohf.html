The map the five sit on.

When Dualis is the brain, the public book has to be on a tap. This plate is that book in short. Not a scoresheet. Not a waiver shop. Official names and jurisdictions only.

Ontario under Hockey Canada

Three branches cover amateur hockey in the province:

OHF

Ontario Hockey Federation. Largest Hockey Canada member here. Seven voting members inside it.

HEO

Hockey Eastern Ontario. Sibling branch. Not an OHF member.

HNO

Hockey Northwestern Ontario. Sibling branch. Not an OHF member.

Seven OHF members

Equal votes. Membership is the book’s list, not a Dualis harvest.

OMHA

Ontario Minor Hockey Association. Minor hockey and associations registered as at 25 June 2005, except Alliance associations, in the area not GTHL and not NOHA. Map changes need a two-thirds Member vote.

ALLIANCE

Minor Hockey Alliance of Ontario. Named association list in the leftover area not GTHL and not NOHA. Own AAA zones. Map changes also need two-thirds.

GTHL

Greater Toronto Hockey League. Minor hockey in Toronto, Mississauga, Vaughan (bounds in the handbook). Shares Markham with OMHA under Lake Ontario Region language.

NOHA

Northern Ontario Hockey Association. North map in the same article. Own championship, then OHF.

OWHA

Ontario Women's Hockey Association. Female hockey in the province, including the OHF area. Own path under the same roof.

OHA

Ontario Hockey Association. Junior book. OHF member. Not a youth-seat road on Ice.

OHL

Ontario Hockey League. Major junior in the OHF area. OHF member. Not a youth-seat road on Ice.

Five youth roads to the provincial weekend

Club / zone → that member’s season → that member’s championship → OHF. Red Hats language belongs to the OMHA road. The other four still meet at OHF. Nobody nests inside OMHA.

AAA, tryouts, waivers

Category (AAA vs AA) is set by the Member. A AAA Zone is a geographic bundle of centres for that category.

A AAA Waiver is written permission from a AAA club so a player may evaluate and register with an adjacent AAA club they are not residence-tied to. Eligibility in the electronic policy: register for that club’s AAA tryouts, attend in the right age, and not have been signed or offered a card there. Dualis cites the object. Dualis does not issue the paper.

Tournaments that are not leagues

Silver Stick is a tournament association, not an OHF member. AA and below run regionals then finals. AAA has no regionals — invite only at Whitby, Sarnia, or Port Huron. A Dualis club is “going” only if that club’s board says so.

What the harvest may write

A member named in the OHF book. A centre or zone named in that member’s current list. A page that answered. A leftover when a cite dies. No invented team. No invented player.
