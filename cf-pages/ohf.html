Why this page exists

If Dualis is the brain, it has to hold the structure that already exists. Ontario is not one league. The OHF is a roof. Five youth members play under it. Two more members are junior. Two other Hockey Canada branches sit beside it. Guessing a sixth youth league, or nesting GTHL under OMHA, is invention.

The roof

The Ontario Hockey Federation is Hockey Canada’s largest Ontario member. It sits beside Hockey Eastern Ontario (HEO) and Hockey Northwestern Ontario (HNO). Those two are not OHF members.

The OHF has seven equal voting Members. New membership is an OHF Board + Member vote. Dualis does not admit a league.

Five youth members — the product to start

OMHA

Ontario Minor Hockey Association. Minor hockey and the associations registered as at 25 June 2005, except Alliance associations, in the area not GTHL and not NOHA. Changing that map needs a two-thirds Member vote. Large. Not the whole province.

ALLIANCE

Minor Hockey Alliance of Ontario. Own named association list in the leftover area not GTHL and not NOHA. Own AAA zones. Own championship. Then OHF.

GTHL

Greater Toronto Hockey League. Minor hockey in Toronto, Mississauga, Vaughan as bounded in the OHF by-laws. Own book. Never an OMHA zone.

NOHA

Northern Ontario Hockey Association. Own map. Own championship. Then OHF. Distance is part of the product. A dead cite is a leftover, not a reason to drop the north.

OWHA

Ontario Women’s Hockey Association. Girls and women. Same roof, own path, own championship lane.

Also OHF members — not youth seats

OHA

Ontario Hockey Association. Junior. Under the roof. Not this youth plate.

OHL

Ontario Hockey League. Major junior. Under the roof. Draft-year talk points here later — still not a U16 Dualis roster.

How a season is supposed to walk

  1. Play inside your member — not a nest under one of the other four.
  2. Win that member’s championship weekend.
  3. Meet at OHF. For OMHA that road is often called Red Hats, then OHF. The name is OMHA’s. The provincial weekend is shared.

AAA zone and waiver — the objects

A AAA Zone is a geographic bundle of Centres so that category can exist. The Member draws it.

A AAA Waiver is written permission from a AAA club so a player may evaluate and register with another AAA club. Only that club’s signing officers issue it. The electronic policy says the player must have registered and attended that club’s tryouts and not have been signed or offered a card there. Non-adjacent jumps need the residential club and every adjacent club. Dualis stores the object. It does not mint a waiver or name a minor.

Tournaments we will cite

If a listing is public and names a level, we bring the event. We do not invent a field or a guest list.

International Silver Stick AAA has no regionals. AAA is invite-only at Whitby, Sarnia, or Port Huron. AA and below win regionals. “Top teams from various leagues and regions” is their sentence, not a Dualis rank. A Dualis club is “going” only when that club’s board says so.

What the brain will not write

  • An invented team to fill a hole.
  • An invented player.
  • GTHL as a child of OMHA.
  • HEO or HNO as OHF members.
  • A Silver Stick AAA regional.
  • A helix lander.

Silence is a leftover. Validated truth is a cite that answered.
