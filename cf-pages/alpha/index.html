03 · Decentralized by design

Build it. Verify it. Let go.

The building agent does not keep a hidden key. The delivered system is meant to be owned, audited, revoked, and continued by a named successor entity.

Successor handoff checklist
  • Export source, compiled assets, checksum, and receipts.
  • Name the successor entity and record the transfer time.
  • Revoke temporary build and connector authority.
  • Verify the successor can reproduce the read-only audit.
  • Keep write authority behind the Father/kernel gate.
