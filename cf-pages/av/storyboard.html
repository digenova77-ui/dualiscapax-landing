Iris audio-video storyboard

This is what the browser can do today. It is not a movie face. It is a living field driven by your mic, her voice, and the chat brain that already exists.

Beat 1 · Silence

The page is quiet. No mic, no camera, no speaker ring. A tap is the only start. That is NO_FORCE.

Beat 2 · Binary wakes

After a tap, a geodesic field lights. Dots move like charge on a wire. This is not a cartoon person. It is the computation becoming visible.

Beat 3 · She occupies space

Iris speaks through the device voice. A 64-point speaker ring places her in the room. Proximity bass rises if you lean in.

Beat 4 · You appear

Camera and screen are jackets you turn on. They stay on this device.

Beat 5 · The mind stays V2

Chat still goes to /v2/chat. The AV jacket does not replace IrisLive. V1 helpers keep working.

Beat 6 · Fuel voice

If the house tank is empty she says: we need more fuel boss if you want to ride any further.

Beat 7 · Leave clean

On page hide, tracks stop and the audio context suspends.

What this is not

Not a filmed human. Not a purchased avatar. Not a sealed KEMAR archive in GitHub Pages. Simulation is not treatment.
