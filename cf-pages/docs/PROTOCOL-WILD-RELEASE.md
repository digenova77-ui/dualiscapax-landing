# Dualis · Wild-release protocol charter

**Status:** founder intent lock · 2026-09-13  
**Not:** an Ontario/Canada company story · a hometown build-out · a savings sales pitch  
**Is:** a protocol that runs by itself in the wild — forever — Bitcoin-shaped release

---

## 0. One sentence

Release Dualis as a decentralized protocol that measures residual (information / value leak) at the point of production, settles without a corporate kill-switch, and keeps running after DualisCapax Inc. is irrelevant.

---

## 1. What must be true (or it is nothing)

1. **No founder-in-the-room dependency.** If it only works when David is physically in Belleville / Ontario / Canada, it is not Dualis.
2. **No human at the top.** External governments and corporations fail here because they require a human apex. Dualis must not recreate that flaw inside the protocol.
   Coercion lemma: any human at the top can be forced (fear for their life) to make 1+1=3. A protocol with no apex cannot be shotgunned into rewriting arithmetic.
3. **No corp kill-switch.** An Ontario (or any) corporation may be an optional husk for early legal/payment seats. The protocol must not require that husk to keep measuring, sealing seats, or settling.
4. **No geography face.** Public law is universal. Cities and nations are seats, not the brand.
5. **On-device books.** Customer data/books never become Dualis cloud property. Receipt hashes only if outbound is ever granted.
6. **Never invent.** DCLM / Residual never stamp fake %, fake partners, or fake savings.

---

## 2. Bitcoin-shaped release

| Bitcoin shape | Dualis shape |
|---|---|
| Soft-launch into the wild | Publish open protocol + reference runtime; anyone can run a seat |
| No CEO can stop the chain | No Dualis officer can stop local measure / seal / settle once released |
| Merchants/exchanges can be pressured | Merchants/integrators can be pressured; the protocol still runs peer-to-peer |
| Fixed rules > company roadmap | Residual Law + Unity ID + seat seal rules ship as versioned protocol |

Enforcement may hit merchants and fiat on-ramps. Expected. What can human governance really do to a protocol with no human apex? Squeeze the edges. The interior keeps running — and overreach tends to prove why the protocol exists.

---

## 3. Stack to absolute max (target, not claimed live)

1. **Unity framework** — identity / seat / household / org binds without a central Dualis account vault.
2. **Residual Law** — measure leak at production; never a moral savings sales pitch.
3. **Atomic swaps** — seat-to-seat / value-for-value without custodial Dualis middle.
4. **Post-quantum path** — seals and swap proofs migrate to PQ-safe primitives.
5. **Governance without a throne** — upgrade/dispute rules that do not collapse to email the Ontario corp or any CEO. Best governance = hard to capture, no human apex.
6. **Autonomous agency** — Iris/agents as protocol services at the seat, not head-office staff.

## 8. Why the company must run itself

Requiring an ultimate handoff to a human government apex is compliance with an illogical law in the pure sense: it recreates the shotgun problem (a human who can be forced to say 1+1=3).

Founder posture: cannot afford to operate Dualis as a conventional policy-compliant corporate throne. The entity must become self-running protocol — optional legal husk at most — so measure/seal/settle do not depend on David forever saying yes to that apex.
