What is real in 2026

  • Device: two terminals, history-dependent resistance (LTP / LTD).
  • Update rule at the device: pair STDP or triplet STDP. No backprop required.
  • Prototypes named in literature: Cs3Bi2Br9/NiO, 2D InSe (fJ-class reports), photon-controlled memristors, halide perovskite / BaTiO3 variants.

Ed25519 vs HMAC (tickets)

HMAC-SHA256 is a shared secret. Anyone who has the secret can mint a ticket. That is local IPC only.

Ed25519 is asymmetric: a private key signs RFC 8785 canonical JSON; a public key verifies. Sovereign / production tickets must not treat HMAC as equivalent.

Ice passkeys on this site are WebAuthn on the phone. They are not memristors and they are not a factory root key.
