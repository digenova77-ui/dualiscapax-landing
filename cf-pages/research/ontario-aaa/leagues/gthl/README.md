**Geography:** Toronto, Vaughan, Markham, Mississauga. Destination minor-hockey market.

**Captured:** 2026-09-12 (America/Toronto)

## Brand notes

- **Own conference.** Never under an OMHA header. Portal priority stays OMHA-first; GTHL is the later / deeper lane.
- Official 2026-27 approved list: **12 AAA clubs × 8 ages = 96 AAA teams** (U10–U16 + U18; no separate U17 AAA club set).
- League site (gthlcanada.com) has standings / news / approved-teams PDF — **no** central public rosters. Club Sportsheadz pages + EP + IG are the pack path when authorized.
- EP jersey coverage is **uneven** (Marlboros strong; Vaughan / Sens names-without-numbers). See `../../GTHL-VS-OMHA-ROSTER-DEPTH.md`. Do not treat GTHL as “EP dump and done.”
- Universe is smaller than OMHA (12 vs 20) — easier **registry** ops, not categorically easier roster fill.
