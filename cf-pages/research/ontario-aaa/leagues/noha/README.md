# NOHA / GNHL AAA — Dualis brand `noha`

**League:** Great North Hockey League (GNHL), under the Northern Ontario Hockey Association  
**Former names:** Great North U18 League (GNU18L, 2024–2026) · Great North Midget League (GNML, through 2024)  
**Geography:** North Bay, Sudbury, Sault Ste. Marie, Timmins, Kapuskasing, New Liskeard / Temiskaming Shores, plus Thunder Bay Kings on the GNHL sheet.  
**Dualis seat prefix:** `on.aaa.noha.{team}.{age}.{season}`  
**Captured:** 2026-09-12 (America/Toronto)

## Brand notes — sparse, own lane

- **Not OMHA North. Not NOJHL junior.** North Bay Battalion / Sudbury Wolves (OHL) / Soo Greyhounds (OHL) / NOJHL clubs are a different universe.
- Rebrand announced 2026-08-04: GNU18L → **Great North Hockey League** because the league now includes **U16 AAA and U18 AAA**. EP still uses the `gnu18l` slug.
- Depth is **sparse** vs GTHL/OMHA. Three U16 programs. Seven U18 programs. No standalone GNHL U14/U15 AAA circuit at capture — younger NOHA AAA shows up as **interlock guests** on other leagues’ sheets.
- 2026-27 NOHA pilot: the three U16 teams play **mostly each other** plus a partial U18 schedule. U16 results do not count toward the U18 championship. New U16 showcase for all U16-aged NOHA players.
