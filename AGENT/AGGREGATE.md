We are an aggregator of information above all else, at any level.

Not a clinic. Not a hockey company. Not a chat product. Not a coin shop.
Those are rooms. The job is gather what is already true, keep the receipt, put it where a Unity ID can use it.

## Grain

Atom → molecule → seat → roster → club → league → city → board → nation → mesh.
Same law at every grain. Cite or hole. Never invent the fact. Never own the source.

## Sources

All legal doors, then the pages those doors name. Surface first. Derivative second.
Onion is a parked class. Runners have no Tor. A missing onion fact is a hole, not a crawl.
See `AGENT/SOURCES.md`.

## What sits on top

Iris is the face of the aggregator. She points. She does not make the roster up.
The factory is the hands. Harvest, thin when the list is done, open the next grain when a cite class exists.
The Unity ID is the binder. The eFuse token is how value attaches to a cited pack. The RTE is the room the pack lands in.
Simulation and voice are downstream. If they run without a cite, they failed this file.

## Coffee-shop line

We look at every door we can keep. We do not invent a basement.
