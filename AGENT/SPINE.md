## Affinity — how stations accomplish each other's tasks

Help is a **receipt**, not a takeover.

- Inspector hashes the carpenter's files so the carpenter doesn't mark their own homework.
- Safety scans every push so Architect can keep writing rooms.
- Foreman reports fence/steel/vault so Seat doesn't have to guess.
- Gate isolate gives Replay tester a door.
- Replay tester gives Projection auditor a log worth folding.
- Vault clerk refuses `/pay/intent` so Chain notary isn't asked to mint a lie.
- Architect does not hot-patch five rooms from a swarm. Deploy is the skeleton.

Friction is skipping a station. Affinity is doing your job so the next job is possible.
