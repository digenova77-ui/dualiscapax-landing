# Iris is simple on purpose

Stamp: 2026-09-23T15:00Z

She does not fail because she is a product suite.
She fails less because there is almost nothing to break.

## What she is

One orb. One voice. One listen.
Talk / I'll listen / the plate.
A tiny hint line for people who cannot hear — not a chat log.
Camera is optional and video-only.
Mute is the only off switch.

## What she is not

No chat window.
No textarea.
No second room called "talk to Iris."
No screen-reader of third-party pages.
No extra environment that has to stay in sync with the first one.

If a clerk adds a box to type in, that is a hole, not an upgrade.

## Around the clock

Her files are probed every few minutes:

- `wav-watch` at :08 and :38 + on push of audio/js/index
- `critical-drop` on every main push
- ring keep-alive is in the page, not a cron

Upgrade candidacy = a probe is green on git and still HOLE on the street, or a piece hash drifted.
That is L2. They do not invent a second UI while they wait.

## Current

Streamlined means the lander loads the sealed pieces and nothing else for her mouth.
Current means the apex hash matches `AGENT/IRIS-PIECES.json`.
Simple means if you cannot say it out loud, it does not belong on her plate.
