## The one walk (six stops)

Think of a shop door with a bell, a guest book, a lock, a ledger, and a till. You do not rebuild the shop. You put the bell, book, lock, ledger, and till in that order.

1. **Hear the room.** Sound is on. The Unity preview is the license: if you click it, the mic may listen so you can say stop, and so you can say play again. No password. No account.
2. **Pick a seat.** For you / Onboarder / C-suite on `/portal.html`. A school board uses `/schools.html`. Government order is country, then province, then the sector desk. Targeted is not signed.
3. **Keep eight words on this device.** `/portal.html` writes the vault with `DCVault`. Dualis never sees the words. Lose them, and this device cannot guess them back.
4. **Optional: lock the vault with this phone's face or finger.** That is the biometric from the v6 spec. It stays on the chip. It does not replace the eight words. The words are how you recover. The face is how you open this phone without typing.
5. **Bring the books you already keep.** A sheet or a link to payroll / tax software. Read in the browser. No cell values leave. Wipe forgets the scan.
6. **See one leftover. Pay your own minutes if it holds.** `/measure.html` and `/payments.html`. Other people pay their own seat. If the number will not invert, the door closes clean.

Iris stays behind the door. You do not meet her to start.
