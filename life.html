A person carries one ID

Unity ID is an access bond at the personal level. It belongs to whoever the person is, wherever they live. DualisCapax is one house that can host a plate. The company does not own the ID.

What the world may see

A public look: a human number, a name they chose to publish, a town. That is the whole internet face.

What stays with the person

Licence, health card, passkey, phrase. Those are not Dualis files. They are shown only to a party the person listed: MTO, a hospital, a physician, a pharmacy — or no one.

How a bond works

Default is deny. A desk that is not on the list gets U1 and nothing else. A desk that is on the list may ask the holder to present a card. Dualis does not copy that card to GitHub or to the public plate.

Why this is bigger than the house

A company can go away. A person still needs one number that means: this is me, and I decide who may ask for more. That is Life on this site. Not a product upsell.
