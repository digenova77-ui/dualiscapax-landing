FAQ

What is this for me?

You already pay leftover — extra steps, extra heat, extra waiting. We name that leftover in your units. If we cannot, you owe nothing.

Do you want my money?

Not as a pitch. We want the money you already burn on waste named, so the rest stays yours. Look is free. Bind is optional and closed until settlement is real.

Do you want my time?

Not as a hold. We want the hours leftover used to eat handed back as a path you can walk without a three-to-five-day queue.

Do you want my goal?

No. We want to give you the next step, then the next, until the goal is still yours.

Do you want my credit?

No. We want tools in your hands so the credit lands on you.

Will you shove this?

No. Walk away whenever you want. We are unused until you use us. That is how we get proven.

Is this an investment?

No. Not shares. Not a return. Research stays readable. Counsel handles anything that is not a measured seat.
