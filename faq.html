FAQ

What is this for me?

You already pay for waste — extra compute, extra protocol steps, extra heat. Dualis measures that residual on your constraint and gives you a number you can check.

What does Basic cost?

Nothing to look. A sandbox seat is free. You pay only if you bind: prepaid depth, or ten percent of savings we can actually validate. If we cannot name the savings in your units, the price stays zero.

Can I buy a seat tonight?

No. Seats are closed until settlement lands. Payments pages write an order ID only. That is not a charge.

Is this an investment?

No. Not shares. Not a return. Research on this site stays readable. Counsel handles anything that is not a measured seat.

Why should I stay?

Because the number is yours. If it cannot be run backwards, it is not Dualis — and you owe nothing.
