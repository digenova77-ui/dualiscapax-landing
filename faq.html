Fuel & Depth

What is Fuel?

Fuel is prepaid capacity for adaptive depth. It pays for real work. It is not a speculative coin.

Is open research free?

Yes. The open surface stays free.

When is Fuel used?

Only for adaptive depth: heavier assistance, analysis, simulation, and sustained sessions that carry real cost.

What happens when Fuel hits zero?

Depth pauses. Open research remains available. Add capacity to continue depth work.

Is Fuel a tradable token?

No. Fuel is closed prepaid capacity for use on the plane.

Residual

What is plane residual?

Residual is the system’s remaining capacity. Every definite action costs residual. It cannot be recreated. It does not sit in a personal wallet.

What is the Singularity Clock?

The residual of the system, not of any single person. Residual on the plane. Not your wallet.

Enterprise

Can an enterprise fund capacity?

Yes. Enterprises can fund prepaid pools for sustained depth. This does not create a private standard or preferred lane.

Do individuals still get Open access?

Yes. Open remains available to everyone under the same published rules.
