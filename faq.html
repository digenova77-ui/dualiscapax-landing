FAQ

Ask it straight

If a fifteen-year-old cannot follow the answer, the answer is wrong. Here are the short ones.

What is this?

A small Canadian studio that builds tools to find leftover waste — extra steps, extra waiting, extra cost — the way you find a leak under a sink. We write the number down. You can check it.

What can I do today?

Read. Talk to Iris, our helper. Give a research gift if you want. You do not need an account to look.

Is Iris a doctor?

No. She is not a doctor, a lawyer, or a bank. She is a helper on this site. If you need care, call a real clinic.

Is Fuel a coin or a stock?

No. Fuel is prepaid minutes for our tools, like putting money on a transit card. What you do not use stays yours. It is not a share. It is not investment advice.

Do I have to pay?

No. Looking is free. Talking to Iris is free. Giving is optional. Buying minutes is optional.

What if the number is wrong?

Then you owe nothing. If we cannot show the same figure again, the deal does not stand. That is the house rule.

Can I leave?

Yes. Close the tab. Walk away. We are only useful if you choose to use us.
