Hypothesis

Forward room can end

We do not know that shrinking distance between Σ₀ and max entropy is t−1 = t. We do not glue them.

We treat the shrinking as a working belief: unused space between origin and the heat wall is getting shorter.

If that belief and that equation ever meet, “back in time” would mean this: past a threshold you do not get another step forward. The playground is too small. That is a horizon. It is not rewind.

What we will not stamp

We will not say the world is running backwards today. We will not say a still is t stopped. We will not spend the last unused grain as if the horizon already closed.
