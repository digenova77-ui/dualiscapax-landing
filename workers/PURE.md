# Workers are pure of DNA / Helix checks

Iris gateway and Stripe fulfill do not read, require, hash, or refuse on DNA or Helix.

Allowed gates:
- DCLM Layer [0]: NO_FORCE, HOST_SAFE, CLEANUP_FIRST, TRUTH_OR_NOTHING
- BYOK / house rail for Iris
- Stripe signature, identity, merchandise jackets for fulfill

Forbidden gates:
- DNA present / missing
- Helix present / missing
- eight-file hash rebuild
- plate assembler

If a future change adds those names as a check, it is a hole. Do not ship it.
