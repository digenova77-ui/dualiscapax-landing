Theoretical limit

The point difference

The model built ahead of time is the ceiling. It is the theoretical limit of what we can offer you: a prediction shaped to your constraints before you sit down.

Real time is the point difference. That gap — model versus what actually happens — is the leftover. That is what we name. That is what you pay to have inverted, or you do not pay.

If the live number matches the model, the difference approaches zero. If it does not, the difference is the work. 1.0 stays unused because a point difference always remains.

For you

You do not buy the universe. You buy the difference between what was predicted for you and what time did. Everything else is costume.
