Onboard here

Start with the leak you already feel.

A shop and a city desk want the same thing: a number they can check before they spend another year. That is the whole door.

Every choice writes a line that time will not erase. Electricity keeps the copy even when nobody is watching. So the figure has to be honest, or you are buying a story.

Why a company wants this

Money leaves the way water leaves a pipe. You do not need a speech about the pipe. You need the drip counted, written down, and turned back into a proof you can hold. If the proof will not invert, you owe nothing. That is how a shop keeps next year’s budget from being a wish.

Why a public desk wants this

A hospital shift, a school board, a plant, a warehouse — the public already paid. The leftover is already a cost. A city desk wants the same invert rule: if the number cannot be walked back to a record, nothing is owed and nobody is sold a miracle.

The path

1 · Bring what you already keepA spreadsheet. A payroll export. A link to the books you use. The file stays on your device. We do not take the book home.
2 · See the leftoverOne leak. One bill, one contract, one line. A figure you can read out loud to a twelve-year-old.
3 · Decide with time prepaidIf the figure holds, you may bind prepaid minutes. Unused time stays with you. Other people pay their own seat.
4 · Stop if it will not invertIf the number cannot be turned back into a proof, the door closes clean. No leftover debt. No story in place of a measure.

The math sits behind this glass. You do not have to speak it to use the door. You only have to know that time moves one way, and a false number will not survive the walk-back.
