Why a company wants this

Money leaves the way water leaves a pipe. You do not need a speech about the pipe. You need the drip counted, written down, and turned back into a proof you can hold. If the proof will not invert, you owe nothing. That is how a shop keeps next year’s budget from being a wish.

Why a public desk wants this

A hospital shift, a school board, a plant, a warehouse — the public already paid. The leftover is already a cost. A city desk wants the same invert rule: if the number cannot be walked back to a record, nothing is owed and nobody is sold a miracle.
