There is one book. What is written stays written. Measuring the book is another write.

First read

Pencil versus pen

A school notebook lets you erase. The universe DualisCapax studies does not. Once a thing happens, the happening is the record. You can write a better next line. You cannot unsay the last one.

That is why leftover matters. The leftover of a line you already wrote still occupies the page.

Fairness

Same book for every seat. House seats 1 to 10 are family and kin. Public seats start at 11. Later seats do not move earlier ones. No tribe gets a private eraser.

Then the grain

The public ledger of DualisCapax is the site you can audit, the hashes you can rerun, and the instruments that name a bind. SRI-1 hashes a completed form to SHA-256 so the signer holds a fingerprint of what they bound. Fuel memos tag a prepaid-time pack to a rail.

Internal architecture stays internal. Public law is simpler: append-only marks, invert before bill, residual as tax, Ontario and Canadian law on the company.

Mid-run measurement is itself an irreversible write. Opening this page is already a mark in your own day. DualisCapax does not pretend a look is free of time. DualisCapax does not charge a look.
