Cost-first gate (non-votable)

INFLOW (service / residual purchase) → [1] Residual cost of that work → [2] Cost-down / continuity (published ratio) → [3] SURPLUS residual income (only if real) → Founder residual ≤ 50% of this surplus → Issued residual ≥ 50% of this surplus → by residual weight

No surplus → no residual income distribution. Weight still stands for later surplus.

Standing weight

  • Onboard under residual rules once — relationship is ongoing.
  • Later payments on the plane do not require a new ticket.
  • Early residual (when unit cost is high) earns stronger weight.
  • Late residual (when unit cost is low) earns weaker marginal weight.
  • Empty registration = zero weight.

Founder residual

  • Surplus residual income hard-capped at ≤ 50%.
  • Illiquid until published checkpoints on the residual race.
  • No discretionary diversion — logic gate runs published ratios.
  • Company sets constitutional ratios; does not steer daily flow.

Admitted assets

Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other playground-admitted assets settle at equal fiat-dollar residual value for weight accounting. Ticker privilege is forbidden. List is compliance-gated (Ontario / Canada first).

Protocol change

Free-will operators must comply with residual rules. Script and ratios change only by defined majority consensus — not one-person rewrite. Constitutional locks (cost-first, anti-print mint, surplus cap, purity) are not soft-votable into corruption.

Rejects

  • Equal cash injection of every payment to every headcount member
  • FOMO prism / recruitment-funded returns
  • Printer dilution of residual weight
  • Founder diversion at will
  • Ticket-per-draw lottery framing
One line. Residual weight is standing surplus-claim earned only by real residual that clears cost-first; early is heavier; founder surplus ≤50% and illiquid until checkpoints; printer cannot mint it; equal headcount of gross never replaces it; only majority consensus moves the script.
