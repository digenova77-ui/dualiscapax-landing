What counts

A hash. A signed page. A warehouse count at the end of a shift. A reading you can repeat. That is information. You can use it to open a lock, settle a claim, or prove a step.

A hope or a slogan is fine to say out loud. It is not enough to bind a seat or move money. Those need a checkable mark.
