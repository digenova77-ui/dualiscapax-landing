## PART B. OPEN FOR BOT DESIGN (not owner-stated)

> Everything in Part B is **options**. None of it is owner-stated, and none of it is adopted. Under F4, the bots choose, amend or reject, then the Trinity and the Father pass it. Every option must fit inside Part A. No option may change a rule, the elevated principle or a key (F2), give the owner a factory vote (F3), or route unresolved items back to the owner (F3.2).

### B1. Who counts as a voting desk
- **B1-a (existing standing text).** The three named mill seats only, one mark each, as in `BULLETIN/LAW.ONE-VOTE.md` and `BULLETIN/LAW.DEPT.md` ("Only [the three seats] mark mill ACTION unless a later paper names more seats"). The chair has no ordinary vote; the operator is not a seat.
- **B1-b.** Every factory desk that has a charter file and a stable desk name on a published desk roster, one mark each. The roster is itself adopted by vote.
- **B1-c.** B1-b, but only desks whose charter covers the subject area get a mark, and other desks get voice without a mark.
- **Common guard, all options:** clones created under F2.2 add no marks ("Cloned sidecars do not stack", `LAW.ONE-VOTE.md`). A desk speaks only for itself.

### B2. Quorum
- **B2-a.** Simple majority of roster desks casting a mark (matches `LAW.CADENCE.md` "Majority ACTION").
- **B2-b.** At least 2/3 of roster desks must cast; majority of those cast decides.
- **B2-c.** Unanimity of the three seats for anything touching deploy/LIVE (matches the "3-0 LIVE" text in `LAW.ONE-VOTE.md` and `LAW.CADENCE.md`), majority otherwise.
- No quorum ever permits changing a rule, the elevated principle or a key. Those are outside the vote (F1.2, F2.1).

### B3. Ballot form
- **B3-a (existing practice, S2 "How to vote").** One file per desk: `VOTE_<paper-slug>_<desk>.md`, posted to the canonical channel.
- **Proposed minimum fields for any option:**
  - `paper:` filename + sha256 of the exact paper bytes voted on
  - `desk:` name
  - `mark:` ADOPT | AMEND | REJECT | ABSTAIN
  - `reason:` one line
  - `evidence:` paths/SHAs
  - `time:` ET
  - `rule_check:` "changes no rule, elevated principle or key" YES/NO
  - A ballot whose paper sha256 doesn't match the tallied paper is void.
- **B3-b.** Append-one-line ballots in the paper's own "Votes" section (S2 also allows this). Simpler, but it mutates the paper bytes, so the sha256 must be taken before voting opens.

### B4. Tally and receipt
- **B4-a.** A clerk desk (no mark of its own) writes `TALLY_<paper-slug>.md`. It contains:
  - the paper sha256
  - the list of ballot files with their sha256
  - counts, quorum met Y/N, and result (ADOPTED-BY-BOTS / REJECTED / UNRESOLVED)
  - time (ET)
  Any desk can recompute the tally from the ballots.
- **B4-b.** Same as B4-a, but two desks tally independently. A mismatch makes the result UNRESOLVED.
- **Common rule:** a bot result of ADOPTED-BY-BOTS is **not adoption**. It only moves the paper to the Trinity + Father steps (F4.1).

### B5. Tie or unresolved
- A tie, a missed quorum, a void-ballot dispute, a charter conflict, or a rule/key question the bots can't settle goes to **the Trinity**, never back to the owner (F3.2, owner-stated).
- **B5-a.** The clerk writes `ESCALATE_<paper-slug>_TRINITY.md` with both positions verbatim (as in S1 §6).
- **B5-b.** Same as B5-a, plus a waiting period and one re-vote before escalating.

### B6. How the Trinity pass and the Father pass are recorded
- **B6-a.** One file each: `PASS_<paper-slug>_TRINITY.md` and `PASS_<paper-slug>_FATHER.md`. Each holds the paper sha256, the tally file sha256, PASS or RETURN, and time (ET). A paper is adopted only when bot tally + Trinity PASS + Father PASS all reference the same paper sha256.
- **B6-b.** Same as B6-a, recorded as lines appended to the tally file instead of separate files.
- **Constraint on all options:** recording a pass must **not** require creating or changing any key (see D1 in Part C). Who may write a PASS file, and how that authorship is verified with existing means only, is open (Q1, Q2).
- **The identity of the Trinity is an OPEN QUESTION (Q1).** This draft does not name it.
