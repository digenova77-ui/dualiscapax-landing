Do not describe what DualisCapax can do.

## What Bond means for them

1. **Your residual is not ignored**  
   Every decision leaves a residual. Yours is measured and priced — not absorbed into open-ended vendor bleed.

2. **Your capacity is yours**  
   You prepay Fuel. Burn is recorded against *your* seat until spent or released under the terms you accepted.

3. **Your exit stays free**  
   You can leave. Cleanup is deterministic. No hostage data, no lock-in theater.

4. **The same law covers you**  
   Corporation, lab, school, or individual — one peg. No preferred tribe above you.

5. **Your intent is not lost**  
   While seats are closed, your residual order is remembered. When access opens, it is matched under published rules you can verify.

6. **You are not buying a promise of return**  
   You are locking capacity for measured residual work. Not securities. Not equity-by-default. Not a yield product.

7. **Open research costs you nothing**  
   You can read published medical, engineering, math, and science surfaces without paying. Adaptive depth is optional and only burns capacity you chose to lock.

## Surface test (mandatory)

Before any copy ships, answer from their chair:

- What does this line do **for me**?
- Does this sound like a firm describing itself, or like a bond with me?
- Would I feel protected, respected, and clear — or sold to?

If the answer is company-centric, rewrite until it is Bond-centric.
