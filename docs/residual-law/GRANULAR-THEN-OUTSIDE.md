# GRANULAR → THEN OUTSIDE VENDOR PARTNERSHIP

**Problem residual method.**

> Work further toward the **more granular** problem until you **cannot bypass** it.  
> Then use **our own engineering** in the layers that exist **outside** subsidiary partnerships with third-party vendors and their service capabilities.

## 1. Order

```
1. Granularize the problem (smaller residual, exact limit)
2. Bypass if possible under their TOS + our law (organ APIv1, Bridges)
3. When cannot bypass inside partnership surface
4. Engineer in Dualis layers OUTSIDE that partnership
   (APIv2, handoff containers, DualisWebComplex, our crypto, our transitions)
```

## 2. Inside vs outside

| Inside subsidiary partnership | Outside (ours) |
|-------------------------------|----------------|
| GitHub/Cloudflare/Google/Squarespace allowed APIs | Dualis APIv2 space |
| Actions, Pages, DNS as they provide | Handoff containers, TDI, Protocol TOS |
| Connected tools | Company envelope, Bridges *we* author |

We do **not** dig deeper into a vendor’s private interior against TOS.  
We step **out** into Dualis-owned residual and make that work.
