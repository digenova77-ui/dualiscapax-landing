Speak capacity, residual cost, governance, and irreversible decisions — not consumer slogans.

| Surface | What it does **for them** |
|---------|---------------------------|
| **Corporate / Financial** | Residual Cost Peg they can audit; seats closed honesty; no preferred-tribe pricing |
| **Payments / Jump-start** | Prepaid Fuel as measured capacity — not open bleed |
| **Founding** | Residual orders remembered while closed; founding window protocol |
| **Treasury / residual reward** | Operating first · surplus deferred · founder residual capped — governance residual |
| **Seal / IP** | Method opacity where Seal is Seal; Open surface complete where published |
| **Prove** | Money + identity boundary before Depth — control residual they understand |

**C-suite language (Bond):**  
*Your residual is priced. Your capacity is measured. One law covers every entity type. You are not buying theater.*

**Do not** pitch C-suite with only encyclopedia warmth and no peg, no seats, no Prove.
