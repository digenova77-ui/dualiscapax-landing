## 3. Identification rule (for any impact story we tell)

- RCT beats matching. Matching beats a story.
- Δ agents / Δ users is **not** a lottery. Name the assumption.
- Replication before headline.
- Hidden confounder walks through PSM. Publish a \(\Gamma\) or do not wear stone.
- External validity: Kenya's agent machine is not "install our app."
