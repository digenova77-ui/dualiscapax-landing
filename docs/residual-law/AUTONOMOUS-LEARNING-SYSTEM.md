## Goal (absolute)

1. **Knows itself** — closed measure on \(\mathbf{U}\), live surfaces, residuals, grants, mail plane, research depth

3. **Adapts** — copy, depth, capacity pricing, automation, bridges under dual-TOS  
4. **Grows with onboarding rate** — more honest demand → more measured capacity allocation; not pyramid growth  
5. **Stays autonomous under law** — agents DEFAULT-SOLVE; Operator only for fused residuals (signature, MAIL_UNITY_CLOSE, GOLD)

$$
\mathrm{Growth} \propto (\Delta \mathrm{Knowledge}_{\mathrm{bound}} + \Delta \mathrm{Onboard\ rate}) \times (1 - F) \times L
