## 1. Definition: Bridge (not embed)

A **Bridge** is a DualisCapax-owned boundary object that:

- Speaks **only** to **allowed** platform surfaces  
- Carries **our** protocol (CLEAR, BIND, LIMIT, \(\mathcal{R}\))  
- **Binds with** platform TOS (respects their rules at the edge)  
- Does **not** implement DualisCapax **into** the platform (we are not their code, their employee process, or their account identity)

$$
\mathrm{Bridge} \subset \mathrm{DualisCapax} \qquad \mathrm{Bridge} \cap \mathrm{PlatformInterior} = \emptyset
$$

We touch **interfaces**. We do not become **their interior**.
