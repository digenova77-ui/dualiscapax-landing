## Editorial rule

The film may communicate ambition, but it must never turn ambition into evidence. Distinguish implemented, tested, conceptual, and unresolved capabilities. Do not show a simulated flow as a live payment or settlement.

## Timed narration and edit

### 00:00–00:25 — The proposition
**Source chapters:** The Honesty Card; The Cosmos.

Every system begins with an idea.

But an idea is not proof. A model is not a machine. And a promise is not a result.

DualisCapax begins with a simple principle: what can be demonstrated should be demonstrated. What remains uncertain should be called uncertain.

This is the Playground and Its Author.

### 00:25–00:55 — The problem
**Source chapters:** The Darkening; Friction, Named; What Is Recovered, and How It Is Shared.
**Picture:** Tighten transitions; move from fragmentation toward connection.

Resources are limited. Computation consumes energy. Decisions have consequences. And valuable work is often difficult to measure, verify, and reward.

The opportunity is not to pretend these constraints disappear. It is to build a system that accounts for them.

A system where the cost of work, the authority to perform it, and the evidence of its completion are not confused.

### 00:55–01:25 — The architecture
**Source chapters:** Three Functions, One Verdict; Standing That Is Earned.
**Picture:** Introduce one label at a time; do not collapse identity, wallet, and authority into one symbol.

At the centre is Trinity: the authorization gate.

UniFi³ and UNITY ID establish the identity passport and its associated wallet relationship.

But identity is not authorization. A wallet signature is not permission.

Every action must pass the governing rules.

Fuel is consumed to perform work. Stake represents ownership of allocated resources. Merit is earned through audited contribution.

DCX belongs to the external economic boundary. Its exact relationship to the fixed ownership ledger remains unresolved.

### 01:25–02:05 — The machine
**Source chapters:** Prove It on Paper First; Every Variable on the Table; Build Last; The Full Corridor.
**Picture:** Animate one clearly labelled hypothetical request through each stage.

Imagine someone requests a computation.

First, the system determines what is being requested. Then it checks whether the action is authorized. It establishes the resource requirement and the cost.

If permission and resources are present, the work may proceed.

The system measures what happened, records the evidence, and checks the result.

Only when the required conditions are satisfied can settlement be accepted.

If authorization fails, execution must not proceed. If evidence is missing, the claim remains unproven.

The record must tell the truth, even when the answer is no.

Those are possibilities to investigate and prove, not capabilities to claim prematurely.

Work should be measurable. Resources should be accounted for. Contribution should be auditable. Authority should never be purchased.

### 02:35–03:00 — The invitation

The Playground is open to possibility.

DualisCapax.

The record stands open.

## Proof corridor — matching engineering acceptance sequence

1. Request: accept a defined, bounded computational task.
2. Identity: establish the UniFi³ / UNITY ID and wallet association.
3. Authorization: independently evaluate the exact action through Trinity.
4. Pricing and resources: bind the quote to the request and resource schedule.
5. Execution: execute only after the required gates pass.
6. Measurement and evidence: persist output digest and resource evidence.
7. Settlement decision: accept or refuse only against the required authorization and evidence.

**Acceptance principle:** a failed or missing gate must produce a refusal, not a success-shaped response. Test-only evidence must never be represented as live production settlement.
