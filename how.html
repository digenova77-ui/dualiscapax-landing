How it works

Four steps. Your papers stay on your phone. The law stays the law.

This is for anyone. You do not need a title. You can stop after step 1.

  1. Look around. Free. No account.
  2. Pick the desk you already have. Country, province, company, shop, home, or just you.
  3. Show your own file — on this device. A spreadsheet or a link you already use. It is not uploaded to us.
  4. Lock this device if you want to stay. Eight words written down here, or the phone’s own passkey. That is the bind. It is not a contract with a ministry or a bank.

Money

We are not a service you sign up for. We are not an investment vehicle. We are information used to see — and if you can, get back — money you have already spent. Leftover. Waste. Double books. Idle time. That is residual finance here. It is not a yield. It is not a share. Dualis does not send you a cheque for joining.

Iris can walk that file with you. Her time is prepaid, like any specialist. Take her reading to a real-world lawyer or accountant if it has to stand outside this house.
