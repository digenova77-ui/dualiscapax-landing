How it works

Four steps. Your papers stay on your phone. The law stays the law.

This is for anyone. You do not need a title. You can stop after step 1. Paying is not a step on this page.

  1. Look around. Free. No account.
  2. Pick the desk you already have. Country, province, company, shop, home, or just you.
  3. Show your own file — on this device. A spreadsheet or a link you already use. It is not uploaded to us.
  4. Lock this device if you want to stay. Eight words written down here, or the phone’s own passkey. That is the bind. It is not a contract with a ministry or a bank.

What you may “gain” is not a payout from us. It is a named leak in costs you already pay — waste, idle time, double books. We do not promise a number. We do not say you will get to zero. We say the hole can be seen.
