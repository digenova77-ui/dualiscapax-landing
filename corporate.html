DualisCapax is a residual-law financial firm. Capacity is measured. Residual is priced. Close is required. Edge Technologies, adaptive AI, and research sit under the same peg — not as a tip jar and not as a securities booth.

Seats closed until pricing and IP protection are locked. Residual order is remembered when access opens. This page is the financial surface language — not a checkout.

What residual law finance is

  • Prepaid capacity — seats fund measured residual (research packs, adaptive runs, structure), not open-ended vendor bleed.
  • Cost peg — burn is recorded against capacity until spent or released under seat terms.
  • One law — corporation, lab, school, or individual under the same peg. No preferred tribe.
  • Ontario & Canada — public surface under domestic law; counsel for any live financing structure.

What it is not

  • An offer to sell securities, tokens, or any investment interest
  • A promised return, dividend, or equity-by-default
  • A storefront checkout on research leaves while access is closed
  • A softer product for larger spend — residual order differs; product quality does not

How money moves when seats open

Settlement is designed as CAD list price with an equal residual crypto rail — parallel rails, not a built-in exchange. Stripe Payment Links for fiat handoff; no secret keys on this site. Jump-start lands on the payments surface; the Residual Cost Peg is the contract language of the seat.
