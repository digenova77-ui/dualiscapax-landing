Mechanics · Bind

The contract must not be a pot.

A famous pool let one bug empty everyone. Recovery meant asking a network to pretend the theft did not happen. Dualis will not build that shape.

Legal layer

Ontario: offer, acceptance, consideration. The ninety-day look is written terms the company accepts by click. Fuel after that is prepaid passes. A hash of those terms can sit on a chain as a receipt. The hash does not replace counsel. Wet ink still for Crown. Submit is submit. A closed pass does not unwind because someone changed their mind.

Technical layer
  • Zero-dollar window holds no visitor coins.
  • Paid Fuel moves on a processor Dualis does not write, then credits our book.
  • Any on-chain piece records the bind. It does not custody a shared treasury.
  • No admin key that can drain. No “upgrade” that becomes a withdrawal.
  • If a payable contract is ever required: audit before one dollar, pause that only stops new binds, formal check of the invert. Still not “unhackable.”
What we refuse

We will not ask a chain to fork so Dualis can take money back. If the design needs a fork to stay solvent, the design is wrong. Rebuild the design.
