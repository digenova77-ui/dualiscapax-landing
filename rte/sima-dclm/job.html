The job
as she said it.

I work on projects that are ultimately implementing EHRs, or sometime upgrading, integrating, etc.

Outside the badge. On the project. Not inside the hospital as staff. Three verbs. That is the whole job until she names a fourth.

Implement — stand a book up where paper or another book lived.

Upgrade — same book, new skin. Sometime. Not every engagement.

Integrate — two systems already paid for still do not talk. People type the join by hand.

What she is not: the vendor. The CIO. The chart. A replacement hire. Contacts here are business doors — role on the SOW, official page — never a patient book.
