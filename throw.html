Mechanics · Invert

The stone becomes the test.

Hatred, anger, sabotage, a probe — take it as criticism. Put it against the book. Find what we had not considered. Close that leftover. That is the whole use of the throw.

What returns to the thrower

A wall that is now that much thicker. A door here that will not open for them. Time they spent on a hole that no longer exists. If they had a bind with us, a closed pass stays closed. That is the residual of the throw.

What does not return

We do not take their keys. We do not touch their house. We do not break a hash to punish them. Dualis is not a raid. If the only answer is to hurt something they own outside this bind, the answer is wrong.
