A “processor” is four machines. Mixing them is how Dualis becomes an exchange by accident.

1 — Quote

Yes, Dualis can price. You do not need 20 markets. Majors (BTC, ETH, USDC, USDT): median of 3–5 liquid books, or one aggregator already doing that work, plus a band — if the print is more than ~1% off the median, refuse. Long-tail ERC-20s do not have 20 real books. If they do not have two or three fills that would actually execute, there is no quote. No quote, no Fuel.

2 — Detect

Yes, Dualis can watch our own addresses (BTCPay-class). Invoice → listen → credit Fuel when the hash confirms. Fee to Dualis: hosting, not 0.5–2%.

3 — Convert

This is the leftover. Turning 300 tokens into BTC/ETH means inventory or a swap route. That is the desk. In Canada, buying/selling/exchanging virtual currency for a customer is the FINTRAC “dealing in virtual currency” shape. Dualis is a software company that credits Fuel. It is not registering as an MSB to save 1% on a $50 pack.

4 — Card / world fiat

Not Dualis. Banxa or MoonPay. They already hold the license and the card network.
