### Bucket 2: FETCH

- The second eye is not the author.

The collapse is the verdict.

- PASS: the fetch confirmed the claim.
- FAIL: the fetch contradicted the claim.
- HOLE: no fetch happened, or the fetch was incomplete.

An empty seat is a HOLE. Never a PASS.
